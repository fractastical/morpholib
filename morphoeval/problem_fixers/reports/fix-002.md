# Problem-Fixer fix-002: OEIS A051023 center-column traceability

## Finding

Prize Problem 2/3 narratives in `models/1984-wolfram-conus-textile-rule30.py` cite OEIS [A051023](https://oeis.org/A051023). The expected first 128 center-column bits live in `datasets/conus/conus_textile_reference.json` under `rule30_prize_benchmarks.oeis_a051023_first_128`, and `self_test()` check 2 asserts equality — but there was no separate, hash-locked registration/resolution pair for audit outside the biology-round `predictions/prediction_registry.json`.

Independent recomputation (same contract as `rule30_prize_diagnostics(steps=1024)`) matches the fixture **128/128 bits**.

## Root cause

Traceability gap only: the benchmark was enforced by an in-script assert against the JSON fixture, not by a timestamped registration artifact with SHA-256 of the expected bit string and a scored resolution record. No bit mismatch was found in the current tree.

## Fix

1. **`predictions/registrations/R30-OEIS-128-01.json`** — Separate registration (not the locked biology registry) with SHA-256 `a4d0748bb05e4a19738a0252b8f87b374385df59d0630c27dffb175d3fa98a5e` over the 128-character concatenated bit string, computation contract (`run_eca` / `rule30_prize_diagnostics`), and falsifier.
2. **`predictions/resolutions/rule30-oeis.json`** — Confirmed resolution with machine-readable `machine_verdict` (0 mismatches, matching SHA-256).
3. **`tests/test_problem_fixer_oeis.py`** — Hermetic pytest that reloads the model path, compares center column[:128], and validates registration/resolution artifacts.

`predictions/prediction_registry.json` and `predictions/score_resolutions.py` were **not** modified; this claim is intentionally outside the biology-round registry.
