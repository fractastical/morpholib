- 1964-steinberg-differential-adhesion-sorting.py:390-394 — in the partial-engulfment regime the swap move adds an external field `dE += 1.8 * ((x1 if t2 == 1 else -x1) + (x2 if t1 == 1 else -x2))` that is not part of the Graner & Glazier (1992) Hamiltonian, so the doublet lens of Panel A3 is imposed rather than selected. Remove the field (or move the asymmetry into the initial condition), show that the lens still forms from gamma_AB > gamma_BM - gamma_AM alone, and add a self-test comparing centroid_offset with and without it.
- 1964-steinberg-differential-adhesion-sorting.py:361-397 and 747-771 — the "Kawasaki" accelerator exchanges the types of two randomly drawn, generally non-adjacent cells (non-local type teleportation) at an uncited temperature 2.5 T. Restrict swaps to face-contacting cell pairs (C[c1, c2] > 0 in 3D; shared 8-neighbour boundary in 2D), cite or remove the 2.5 factor, and add the 2D ablation (`cell_swaps=False`) so alpha_2D = 0.189 and the log-decay r = -0.877 are reported with and without the accelerator as the 3D run already does.
- 1964-steinberg-differential-adhesion-sorting.py:652-654 with measurement potts_3d_mean_cell_volume_fraction = 0.771 — lambda_V = 2 at T = 30 is too weak against the 26-neighbour adhesion scale, so 3D cells sit 23% below V_0 (min 31 voxels). - 1964-steinberg-differential-adhesion-sorting.py:486-525 and datasets/differential_adhesion_sorting/differential_adhesion_sorting_reference.json:244-247 — the exponent fits include the 0..~10 MCS transient and the two laws are not discriminated (3D R^2 0.9965 vs 0.9845; 2D 0.737 vs 0.769), while the only reference exponent is the LSW 1/3 bound and the Nakajima & Ishihara (2011) value is null.
