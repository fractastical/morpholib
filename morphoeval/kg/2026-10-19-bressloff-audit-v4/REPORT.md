## Findings and remediation

### Bressloff et al. (2001/2002) V1 Euclidean Symmetry-Breaking & Klüver Form Constants  —  overall 70.6 (mostly faithful)

The script is a serious, well-instrumented implementation of the Bressloff et al. (2001/2002) orientation- hypercolumn model: the local/lateral Fourier-Bessel harmonics, the perturbative even/odd dispersion curves, the exact diagonalisation of the orientation operator (which genuinely derives the m=0 non-contoured vs m=1 contoured regime for the auto presets), the sigmoid-expansion weights, the eta-aware hexagon/roll thresholds and the two-regime log-polar map all check out against the papers as I know them, and the retinal pullbacks of the tunnel and the chiral, seamless spiral are convincing. Its weak points are structural rather than cosmetic: the "full cubic" quadratic-feedback path is a reconstruction that is unusable (subcritical / near-resonant) at every sigma0 != 1/2 in the very regime the gallery renders, the badged honeycomb is actually drawn with wavevectors at +-114.6 deg because of torus quantisation, the 1e-15 parity is with a Rust port rather than with any digitised paper figure, and the only empirical comparisons are the paper's own 30-36 stripe / 2.4-3.2 mm statements and Horton & Hoyt's magnification law, neither of which the parity presets meet (lambda_c 9.0 mm, 3.2x too long). Provenance (DOIs, PMIDs, SHA-256 pins, lock file, 40 passing self-test checks, no RNG) is strong, but the README section describing the script is stale relative to this Pass-4 code.
