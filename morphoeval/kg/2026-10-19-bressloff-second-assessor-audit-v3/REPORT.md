## Findings and remediation

### Bressloff et al. (2001/2002) V1 Euclidean Symmetry-Breaking & Klüver Form Constants  —  overall 80.9 (faithful)

Blinded second-assessor pass. 2001-bressloff-v1-form-constants.py is a transparent, machine-precision Python port of the upstream MesmerPrism/bressloff-v1-form-constants Rust implementation covering perturbative Fourier- Bessel marginal stability, local-overlap cubic branch selection, the two-regime log-polar retino-cortical pullback with tangent orientation line elements, parameter sweeps, and an empirical magnification comparison against Horton & Hoyt (1991). While the script exemplifies honest scientific reporting by badging every gallery panel (SELECTED vs STABLE-coexisting vs IMPOSED-unstable), surfacing all 12 failed paper-expectation checks across the 5 stability reports and 24 preset runs, and quantifying its 3.1x cortical wavelength discrepancy against Bressloff et al. (2002), substantive modelling gaps remain. Specifically, the simplified local- orientation cubic integrals never stabilize rolls or squares anywhere in parameter space (requiring 3 of Klüver's 4 form constants to be manually imposed at identical coupling parameters), contoured planforms maximize |a(r, phi)| rather than signed activity a(r, phi), non-axis-aligned cortical modes are not quantized to integer azimuthal cycles on the y-periodic torus, and retinal_pullback_diagnostics returns NaN on y-even lattices (Cobweb and Honeycomb) while mislabelling them as y-independent.

- **Model fidelity to source: 76** — The script accurately implements the intra-hypercolumn Fourier harmonics (Eq. 3.12), anisotropic Fourier- Bessel lateral coupling weights (Eq. 4.9), second-order Rayleigh-Schrödinger dispersion curves lambda_even/odd(q), and the exact two-regime log-polar retino-cortical map with analytic inverse and tangent orientation map phi_R = phi + theta_R, matching all 5 stability_reports and all 24 preset runs in paper- calibration.json to 2.22e-16. However, amplitude_gamma2 and amplitude_gamma3 compute only unweighted local orientation overlap integrals without the spatial lateral-kernel weighting, quadratic-mode feedback, or quintic terms of Bressloff et al. (2001 §5, Eqs. 5.12-5.15), which prevents rolls and squares from ever stabilizing, leaves odd-branch H0/Hpi degenerate (eta_hex = 0), and causes 12 of 133 paper-expectation checks to fail (-10 pts). In stability_scan and branch_selection_for, presets with fixed parity != critical_branch (such as fig29_* and fig30_*) evaluate the even eigenfunction at the odd branch's critical_q and growth rate, and roll_stable checks 2*gamma_3(theta) > gamma_0 only at the preset's rendering angle pattern_angle_deg rather than across all theta (falsely reporting roll_stable=True in CSV rows 9, 10, and 12) (-5 pts). Furthermore, _eval_xy maximizes |a(r, phi)| rather than signed activity a(r, phi), introducing sign-flip jumps across nodal lines that double the contoured hexagonal spatial frequency (k* = 21 vs 11 cycles), and orientation_eigen_details truncates at m=4 with an ad-hoc +-1.5 clamp vs m=10 in branch_coupling_sum (-5 pts).
