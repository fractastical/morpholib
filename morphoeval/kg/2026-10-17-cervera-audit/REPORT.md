## Findings and remediation

### Cervera–Levin–Mafe gap-junction reaction–diffusion and bioelectric synthesis  —  overall 44.9 (partially faithful)

A deterministic synthesis combines a one-dimensional antagonistic reaction–diffusion solver, an orthogonal one-species profile, a Python port of rusty-matter's educational five-band circuit, 14 curated PlanformDB outcome records, and two decoded Planform cartoons. The GIF paints red/blue territories on a photographic silhouette and switches between wild-type and double-head cartoons; the additional outputs are cervera_levin_mafe_bioelectric_morphospace.png and cervera_levin_mafe_metrics.csv. The high-level 2021 premise is recognizable, but equation-level fidelity could not be checked from the inaccessible full paper, the f3 axis has no computational effect, several biological mappings are uncited choices, and the CSV doubles the actual crop wavefront displacement.

- **Model fidelity to source: 38** — The accessible 2021 abstract supports two antagonistic morphogens along one axis, an independent morphogen along an orthogonal axis, and pattern changes under partial GJ blockade, which the two solver functions qualitatively represent. The full paper was not accessible, so mutual annihilation, end-source discretization, boundary treatment, numerical constants and the choice to reduce only D2 cannot be credited as the published equations. More seriously, the claimed (f2,f3) morphospace never uses f3, and its phase boundary is manually drawn. The rusty-matter circuit matches its own pinned educational Rust source, but that source explicitly uses normalized teaching states and is not evidence that this is the 2020 paper's ion-channel model; agreement with its synthetic traces validates a port, not planarian biology.  
  evidence: `2021-Cervera–Levin–Mafe.py` L186–238; `2021-Cervera–Levin–Mafe.py` L241–273; `2021-Cervera–Levin–Mafe.py` L360–599; `2021-Cervera–Levin–Mafe.py` L936–943; `datasets/planform/README.md` L21–27
- **Morphological realism / specimen match: 38** — The territory panel has the recognizable outline of a real Schmidtea mediterranea and the wild-type versus double-head cartoons are visibly distinct in posterior colour and organs. Neither is a generated specimen: the photograph supplies only a binary mask, the morphogens are painted onto it, and a hard readout threshold chooses one of two pre-existing Planform cartoons. No treated or double-head specimen, nor a source-paper morphology panel, is shown side by side. The synthesis PNG also has overlapping panel titles and legends, reducing interpretability, while the GIF's only anatomical change is the categorical cartoon switch.

- **Parameter expressivity: 35** — Lowering f2 produces a visible, monotonic wavefront displacement in the plotted range, and the GIF's circuit readout rises enough to trigger a distinct cartoon. The second advertised morphospace parameter f3 has exactly no effect because its loop variable is unused. Crop-specific f2 values and wound gains are fixed lookup tables rather than exposed, independently swept parameters, and the anatomical output is binary at an unsupported 0.55 threshold. Therefore only one of the nominal two reaction–diffusion dimensions demonstrates the criterion.

- **Morphometric validation: 25** — The figure includes real PlanformDB outcome frequencies and the CSV reports circuit states and wavefronts, which is more than an unmeasured illustration. The silhouette IoU compares two decoded cartoons with each other, not either cartoon with a specimen. Finally, each crop CSV wavefront is reconstructed with a 0.26 multiplier after the shift was normalized by 0.13, so, for example, f2=0.92 is reported as 0.5153 although the solver gives 0.5076; the quantitative output therefore does not faithfully report its own simulation.

Remediation:
- Obtain the full 2021 paper and map every reaction term, boundary condition, parameter and blocker factor to its equation, table or figure; label the implementation a qualitative analogue if those cannot be matched.
- Implement f3 in the orthogonal model and derive the two-dimensional phenotype rule from the published morphogen-to-shape mapping; otherwise remove the f3 axis and phase-boundary claim.
- Replace the hand-set crop f2 values, wound gains, time conversion and 0.50/0.55/0.58 thresholds with source-derived or fitted values, documenting training versus held-out records.
- Correct the CSV to write the direct x_star returned by the solver, and add tests that independently recompute every CSV field and fail when either morphospace axis is inert.
- Compare generated head/auricle morphometrics with treated specimens or source-paper figures; use the photograph as measurable geometry rather than only a backdrop.
- Expand trace parity to every sampled value and distinguish numerical port parity from biological validation; test the reaction–diffusion solver and all figure annotations separately.
