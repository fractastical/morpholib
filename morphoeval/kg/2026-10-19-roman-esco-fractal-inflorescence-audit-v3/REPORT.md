Remediation:
- 2021-pringle-roman-esco-fractal-inflorescence.py:818-831 and datasets/roman_esco_fractal_inflorescence/roman_esco_fractal_inflorescence_reference.json:68 — beta_emergent = (k / r_p(R0)) v_r = 1.81 x 0.16 = 0.289 is 3.4x the reference 0.085; the vendored v_r = 0.16 is untraced and the normalised slope 1.81 exceeds the pure-perimeter value 1.0 because the cell count (and hence initiation sites) scales with surface area.  Vendor the paper's meristem-size vs time data (Azpeitia 2021 Fig. 3) to fix v_r, and make the mesh cell size physical so that k is perimeter- not area-limited.
- 2021-pringle-roman-esco-fractal-inflorescence.py:637-777 and 1252-1255 — emergent divergence 133.9 deg with MAD 25.6 deg (32% within +/-15 deg of golden) is too imprecise to carry Fibonacci parastichies: the coupled tree measures (8,16).  Replace Euclidean sink distances with geodesic distances on the cone, use a structured (hexagonal) L1 mesh with cell division instead of random re-insertion, adopt the Smith (2006) exponential PIN allocation, and target MAD < 10 deg before coupling the angle.
- 2021-pringle-roman-esco-fractal-inflorescence.py:388 and 1312-1327 — H/R(beta) = 0.56 + 10.8235 beta is prescribed and then "validated" (Pearson r = 1.0) against a vendored series that is a linear interpolation of the same end-points.  Derive the envelope from the growth model (H = dz x N(T) with the vendored internode dz values and R(t) = R0 + v_r t) and drop or relabel the r = 1.0 measurement.
- 2021-pringle-roman-esco-fractal-inflorescence.py:415-441 and 482-518 — the 4-order tree is a point skeleton whose children overlap (child_scale 0.38-0.52 with 20-37 children per parent), so its raw box-count D_f is 1.95 (Romanesco) / 1.62 (Cauliflower) against 2.71 / 2.38.  Build an explicit curd surface (union of cones/spheres rendered to a voxel grid) and box-count that surface; independently re-verify or replace the Kim (2004) PRE 70:046109 reference (paper_citations.bib:50-60).
- 2021-pringle-roman-esco-fractal-inflorescence.py:896-923 — specimen H/R 1.76 is a raw image-plane bounding-box ratio with unknown camera elevation, and lambda = 0.390 is conditioned by the fixed LoG scales (7/18 = 0.389) and NMS windows.  Add a second, side-view specimen or a calibrated projection model, and replace the two fixed scales by scale-space LoG with per-blob scale selection so the order-2 / order-3 radii (and lambda) emerge.
- 2021-pringle-roman-esco-fractal-inflorescence.py:163-261 — the GRN is a continuous Hill ODE with uncited hand-tuned coefficients reproducing vendored steady states; Azpeitia et al. (2021) use a logical network.  Implement the paper's logical GRN (or cite a published continuous reduction) and validate against its reported attractor table rather than against Pass 1 target values.
- 2021-pringle-roman-esco-fractal-inflorescence.py:1486 and 1493 — `--r0` and `--seed` are parsed but not wired; either pass them through compute_all (r0 into the plastochron law, seed into compute_3d_box_counting_dimension and the PIN1 mesh) or remove them.
- datasets/roman_esco_fractal_inflorescence/roman_esco_fractal_inflorescence_reference.json:74-165 — the beta sweep series and genotype benchmarks are Pass 1 interpolations; mark them `synthetic: true` with the interpolation rule inside the JSON (not only in the README) or replace them with values digitised from Azpeitia et al. (2021) Figs. 3-4.
