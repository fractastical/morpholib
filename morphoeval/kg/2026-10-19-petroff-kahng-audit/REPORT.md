## Findings and remediation

### Petroff et al. (2010) Conical Stromatolite Spacing Law & Dome-to-Cone Morphospace  —  overall 43.6 (partially faithful)

`2010-petroff-stromatolite.py` implements Petroff et al.'s (2010) diffusion-competition spacing formula `ell = alpha * sqrt(D * tau)` (`ell = 0.66 cm` at `tau = 12 h`, `D = 1e-5 cm^2/s`, `alpha = 1.0`), renders a hexagonal packing diagram, and sweeps `beta = v/lambda` across `{0.3, 1.5, 5.0}` (and `0.2 -> 6.0` in the 70-frame GIF) to illustrate the transition from squat domes (`H/W = 0.67`) to tall cones (`H/W = 2.62`). However, the lamina growth model is not Petroff et al.'s diffusive-flux solver (`nabla^2 c = 0` with growth proportional to normal flux `dc/dn`), nor is it an exact integration of the Batchelor et al. (2004) KPZ equation printed in the docstring: it advances a pinned-base Lagrangian front with an unsourced quadratic crest-weighting `w_photic = 0.12 + 0.88*(z/z_max)^2` and 3-point smoothing, and compares against no empirical stromatolite thin sections or measured spacing distributions.

- **Model fidelity to source: 44** — Correctly evaluates the diffusive spacing relation `ell = alpha * sqrt(D * tau)` (L64-67) and hexagonal lattice geometry (L70-81). However, (-22) the lamina cross-section model (L113-163) does not solve Petroff et al.'s (2010) diffusive nutrient field (`nabla^2 c = 0`) or flux-driven lamina growth (`v_n prop dc/dn`), so the top row (diffusion spacing) and bottom row (lamina shapes) are mathematically uncoupled; (-14) relative to the Batchelor et al. (2004) equation cited in the docstring (`dh/dt = v + lambda*(1 + 1/2 (dh/dr)^2)`, L34, L296), `_advance_front` introduces an uncited quadratic photic crest weight `w_photic = 0.12 + 0.88 * (z / z.max())**2` (L130), pins the basal endpoints at `+-base_half` (L136-137), and applies a 3-point Laplacian smoothing filter (L141-142); and (-8) the text panel states that `ell ~ 0.66 cm` records a `~20 h` rhythmic metabolism (L238-239) whereas `0.66 cm` was computed from `tau = 12 h` (L193, L235).  
  evidence: `2010-petroff-stromatolite.py` L64–81; `2010-petroff-stromatolite.py` L113–143; `2010-petroff-stromatolite.py` L233–240

### Kahng, Odle & Wakeman (2024) Reef-Coral Colony Geometry & Surface-Area Productivity  —  overall 60.2 (mostly faithful)

`2024-kahng-coral-geometry.py` faithfully encodes the discrete annual volume-accretion master rule `V(t+1) = V(t) + G*S(t)/rho` and the four idealized geometric families from Kahng, Odle & Wakeman (2024) (hemispheroid, cone, flat disc, and cylinder), reproducing their surface-area productivity (`SAP = dS/S`), linear extension (`C`), aspect-ratio dependence (Fig. 3), diameter/thickness scaling, and the ~19x microfragmentation SAP speedup for massive *Porites* (`r = 1 cm` vs `r = 25 cm`). Its main limitations are that the finite 1-year step introduces small-radius curvature in hemisphere/cone extension (`C = 0.52 -> 1.25 cm/yr` vs the exact continuum `dr/dt = G/rho = 1.25 cm/yr`), the equal-`G, rho` comparison applies `G = 1.50 g cm^-2 yr^-1` to a thin `h = 0.5 cm` disc (producing `26 cm/yr` extension at `r = 30 cm`), and no empirical colony growth tables or coral specimen photographs are overlaid.

- **Model fidelity to source: 74** — All seven algebraic functions (`hemisphere_extension`, `hemisphere_sap`, `disc_extension`, `disc_sap`, `cone_extension`, `cone_sap`, `cylinder_sap`, L68-110) are exact consequences of the paper's discrete annual volume rule `V(t+1) = V(t) + G*S(t)/rho` under the stated surface/volume formulas (`V = 2/3 pi r^3, S = 2 pi r^2`; `V = h pi r^2, S = pi r^2`; `V = pi h^3/(3 alpha^2), S = pi h^2 sqrt(1+alpha^2)/alpha^2`; `V = pi r^2 L, S = 2 pi r L`). Deduct 14 points because the script does not contrast the finite 1-yr step (`r(t+1) - r(t)`, which rises from `0.52` to `1.20 cm/yr` in Panel c) against the instantaneous continuum limit `dr/dt = G/rho = 1.25 cm/yr` (where hemisphere linear extension is strictly size-independent); and deduct 12 points because general oblate/prolate hemispheroids and multi-branch colony self-shading are simplified to a spherical hemisphere and single cylinder without explicit boundary notes.  
  evidence: `2024-kahng-coral-geometry.py` L57–60; `2024-kahng-coral-geometry.py` L68–110; `2024-kahng-coral-geometry.py` L172–185
- **Morphological realism / specimen match: 42** — Panel (a) draws clear 2D geometric glyphs annotated with `(V, S, S:V trend)` for the four coral growth forms, Panels (b-e) reproduce the analytical curves of Kahng et al. (2024) (including Fig. 3 cone aspect ratios and Fig. 6 shape comparisons), and the GIF animates 15 years of hemisphere and cone cross-sectional growth alongside log surface-area trajectories. Deduct 35 points because no real coral colony photographs, CT-scanned 3D colony meshes, or digitized empirical data points from *Porites*, *Acropora*, or *Leptoseris* are overlaid; and deduct 23 points because in Panel (c) and the GIF, applying `COMMON` (`G=1.50, rho=1.20`) to a `0.5 cm` flat disc drives disc extension to `26.1 cm/yr` at `r = 30 cm` and area to `~10^9 cm^2` at year 15, visually dwarfing the realistic hemisphere and cone curves.  
  evidence: `kahng_coral_geometry_morphospace.png` L0–0; `docs/media/kahng_coral_geometry_growth.gif` L0–0; `2024-kahng-coral-geometry.py` L116–147
