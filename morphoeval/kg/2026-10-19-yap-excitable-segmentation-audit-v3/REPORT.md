- 2016-hubaud-yap-excitable-segmentation.py:447-456 — the 200-cell ensemble (y_samples, j_samples, osc_mask) is computed but `pred_frac` is a logistic of the deterministic I_eff with a hand-set detection margin (0.115) and width (0.125), so the 6-regime Pearson r = 0.9976 reflects two tuned constants rather than ensemble dynamics. - datasets/yap_excitable_segmentation/yap_excitable_segmentation_reference.json:50-58 and 2016-hubaud-yap-excitable-segmentation.py:1173-1214 — the vendored SEM is a chick embryo ('Aves_Neural_tube_and_somites_in_chick_embryo.jpg') filed as mouse_psm_somites_specimen.jpg, with no um per px scale, so neither the organism nor the 100 um stripe spacing can be compared with it. Add a correctly named mouse PSM specimen (or relabel as chick and change the organism claim), record a scale, and compare S_model (um) with the specimen in um.
- 2016-hubaud-yap-excitable-segmentation.py:720-748 — the FGF/Wnt gate g(F) = F^h/(F_thr^h + F^h) with F_thr = 0.15, h = 4 and gamma_F = 1.6 is a phenomenological additive term on I_eff; gamma_F had to be tuned from 0.8 to 1.6 for the excitable pulses to arrest at the front. Replace it with (or calibrate it against) an explicit Fgf8/Wnt3a -> Hes7/Lfng input taken from a cited model, and vendor the Dubrulle (2001) fgf8 gradient profile as a fixture.
- 2016-hubaud-yap-excitable-segmentation.py:750-886 — the clock is quasi-synchronous across the PSM and only slows within ~50 um of the front (Panel G), so the Lauschke (2013) phase-gradient / kinematic travelling waves are not reproduced. Add a posterior-to-anterior period gradient (e.g. tau_v(x) or I_eff(x) ramp) and measure the wave speed and phase-gradient wavelength against Lauschke et al. (2013) mPSM values.
- 2016-hubaud-yap-excitable-segmentation.py:957-965 and 1000-1031 — the cascade constants (E_50 = 10 kPa, A_50 = 500 um^2, m = 3, k_exp = 1/12, k_PP = 1/20, tau_actin = 8 min, 2x Yap5SA overexpression) and the per-regime mechanical inputs are uncited modelling assignments, and the tension-only cascade cannot reproduce the non-monotonic Y-27632 (0.30) vs high-density mPSM (0.18) pair (RMSE floor 0.054). Vendor Dupont (2011) / Wada (2011) measured values, add a density / contact-inhibition term, and store the fitted (alpha_T, kappa_LATS, T_L) as a pinned fixture.
- 2016-hubaud-yap-excitable-segmentation.py:687-701 and 621-684 — the J_max -> Kuramoto R sweep is not strictly monotone (it saturates: 0.802 at J_max = 0.20 vs 0.801 at 0.30, Spearman 0.94) and the phase is an atan2 around the fixed point rather than a protophase-corrected phase; use a Hilbert / protophase estimator, average over several seeds and heterogeneity levels, and compare with Riedel-Kruse (2007) or Hubaud (2017) dissociation-reaggregation synchrony data.
