## Findings and remediation

### Cervera–Levin–Mafe gap-junction reaction–diffusion and bioelectric synthesis  —  overall 55.6 (partially faithful)

A deterministic synthesis combines an explicitly labelled qualitative reaction–diffusion analogue, an orthogonal m3 profile, an educational rusty-matter circuit port, 14 curated PlanformDB records, a one-parameter crop-position fit and two fixed Planform cartoons. The additional outputs are cervera_levin_mafe_bioelectric_morphospace.png and cervera_levin_mafe_metrics.csv. The inert f3 and incorrect crop-wavefront defects are fixed, the silhouette is quantitatively sampled, and the empirical comparison now reports training and held-out errors. Remaining limits are the inaccessible full model papers, no f3-to-anatomy mapping, an uncited phenotype decision rule, weak held-out accuracy, fixed cartoon outcomes, illustrative time mapping and an incorrect reported f2 threshold caused by clipping in its inverse.

- **Model fidelity to source: 45** — The program now accurately describes itself as a qualitative analogue built from the abstract and presentation, and explicitly disclaims equation, boundary-condition and parameter fidelity. Its high-level structure remains consistent with accessible source text: two antagonistic axial signals, an independent orthogonal signal and reduced effective diffusion under GJ block. f3 now genuinely changes D3 and an ML profile, replacing the false 2D heatmap with two honest independent curves. This still cannot score as a reproduction of the published model: the full 2021 paper was unavailable, the local solver docstring still says it implements the paper's system, no published equation or constants are verified, and m3 is not coupled to a morphological outcome. The rusty-matter port remains a separate unpublished educational model rather than the cited 2020 ion-channel model.

Wild type and double head remain visibly distinct. But the body outline never changes, no real treated or double-head specimen or source-paper morphology is shown, and the model does not generate head or auricle form. Crossing a threshold merely swaps between two pre-existing Planform cartoons; measuring coloured territory area improves quantification, not anatomical realism. Thus the central specimen-match requirement remains mostly unmet.

The circuit's crop-specific wound gains also remain a fixed lookup rather than an independently tested parameterization.

- **Morphometric validation: 48** — This dimension has the clearest improvement. A one-parameter f2=1-a p model is fit to four octanol crop positions and evaluated, without refitting, against four innexin-RNAi positions; training RMSE is 4.2 points and held-out RMSE is 23.5 with r=0.94. The code reports these values in the figure and CSV, writes direct solver wavefronts, and measures anterior territory area on the photographic silhouette. This is a real model-data comparison rather than decorative anchors. It remains below the midpoint because four related positions are a very small validation set, no sampling uncertainty or baseline is reported, the 23.5-point held-out error is substantial, and the target is phenotype frequency while the predictor is a normalized signal-boundary index. The silhouette quantity is measured on a real body but is not compared with a measured treated specimen.

Remediation:
- Obtain the full 2021 paper and map the analogue against its actual equations, boundary conditions, blocker factors and parameter definitions; retain the analogue label for every unmatched element.
- Replace the pharynx-crossing decision rule with a cited or empirically tested morphogen-to-phenotype mapping; if retaining this geometry, use actual body-mask bounds and state explicitly that the rule itself is an assumption.
- Fix f2_for_shift so it solves wavefront_for_f2(f2) = x_star_b without a clipped objective, test the recovered crossing, and correct the CSV/panel value from 0.20 to the actual approximately 0.42.
- Validate the crop model on more independent experiments, include sampling uncertainty and simple baselines, and explain whether a 23.5-point held-out RMSE is adequate for the intended claim.
- Map f3 to a measured lateral morphology such as auricle width or head shape and compare it with specimens or source figures; otherwise describe f3 only as signal-profile expressivity.
- Calibrate or remove the illustrative 24-hour circuit overlay, generate legend values and pulse bounds from the simulation, and either source or remove the crop-specific rusty-matter wound-gain lookup.
