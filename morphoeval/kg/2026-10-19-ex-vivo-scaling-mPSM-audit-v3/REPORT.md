### Lauschke, Tsiairis, François & Aulehla (2013) scale-invariant phase-gradient encoding in ex vivo monolayer presomitic mesoderm (mPSM)  —  overall 89.9 (faithful)

Pass 3 remediation-round evaluation of `2014-lauschke-ex-vivo-scaling-mPSM.py` (89.9/100, down from 91.4 in Pass 2 because the round exposed and now prices defects that Pass 2 did not score). Both Pass 2 remediation items are closed: (1) an explicit FGF/Wnt source-sink signalling gradient whose half-length scales with R(t) (exponent 1.000 vs 0.000 for a Dubrulle-Pourquie fixed-length decay gradient) drives a dynamic wavelength relaxation d lambda/dt = -(lambda - c l_half)/tau_lambda with fixed-window segment gating, so alpha = S_n/R(t_n) is now an output: 0.1103 at cycle 8 and mean Phi = 6.125 rad (2.5% from 2*pi) for tau_lambda = T_osc/4, drifting to alpha_8 = 0.5601 when adaptation is frozen and to constant 39.5 um segments with the fixed-length gradient (Panel G, tau_lambda sweep); (2) a Tsiairis & Aulehla (2016) disordered reaggregate lattice of 1577 Kuramoto-Sakaguchi cells with spatially correlated frequency heterogeneity in which phase-gradient flow-source detection finds median 4 de novo target-wave pacemakers in cycles 1-3 consolidating to 1 (17.7 um from the local frequency maximum) by cycles 12-14, with K = 0 and eta = 0 ablations giving 0 pacemakers and K / eta sweeps (Panels H-I, new reaggregate GIF). Six machine- checked Lean 4 theorems (3 new, 0 sorrys), 10 reference-anchored measurements (5 new) and 18 new self-test assertions back the additions. Remaining defects: the vendored "mouse" specimen is a chick SEM, the calibration of c_lambda and delta_phi_seg partly builds the invariant back in, the gradient is quasi-static with a placeholder l_k, fully random initial phases pin spiral defects, the 4-explant "observed" columns lack digitisation provenance and the --R0/--alpha/--T-osc/--seed flags do not reach the default render.

- **Model fidelity to source: 93** — Laws 1-3 are implemented as in Pass 2 (phi = omega_0 t - Phi_0 (r/R)^p, S_n = alpha R_n, R_{n+1} = (1-alpha) R_n, v = (2 pi/Phi_0) R/T_osc; L178-544). Pass 3 adds the mechanism the audit asked for: a quasi-static FGF/Wnt source-sink gradient W = W_0 sinh((R-r)/l_k)/sinh(R/l_k) with the fixed-length exponential alternative (L552-602), a wavelength relaxation ODE toward c_lambda l_half(t) with Phi(t) = 2 pi R/lambda and fixed-window gating S_n = R[1-(1-delta_phi_seg/Phi)^(1/p)] (L605-671), and a Kuramoto-Sakaguchi reaggregate lattice whose continuum limit is Kuramoto's nonlinear phase diffusion equation (L724-931), each with a machine-checked Lean statement (scale covariance, contraction, monotone dispersion). Deductions: c_lambda = (2 pi/Phi_0)/(1/2) and delta_phi_seg = Phi_0[1-(1-alpha)^p] are calibrated so that the adapted fixed point reproduces Phi_0 and alpha exactly, so the invariant is recovered rather than predicted (-3); the gradient is not time-integrated on the moving boundary and l_k = 1e6 um is a placeholder for the sink-dominated limit rather than measured FGF8/Wnt3a D and k (-2); the Sakaguchi lag a = 0.6 rad stands in for a Notch coupling delay without a delay-differential derivation (-2). Net 93 (-1 vs Pass 2: more mechanism, but the new constants are fitted to the law they are meant to explain).
