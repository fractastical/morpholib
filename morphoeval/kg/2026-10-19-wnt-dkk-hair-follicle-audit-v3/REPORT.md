Remediation:
- 2002-sick-wnt-dkk-hair-follicle.py:600-603 — the K14-Wnt 'overexpression' preset changes kappa (0.02 -> 0.015), D_w (0.015 -> 0.008) and D_h (0.45 -> 0.32) together with rho_w, so the 2.0x density is a lattice-scale preset, not a WNT-overexpression perturbation. Add a pure rho_w sweep at fixed D_w/D_h/kappa (and a c_w sweep) and report how placode count, area and NN spacing respond; keep the preset only as a labelled 'compressed-scale' control.
- 2002-sick-wnt-dkk-hair-follicle.py:974-1042 and 1570-1579 — the vendored specimen is a Gad1 in situ of vibrissa/hair placodes (Maddox & Condie 2004) and its NN-spacing CV (0.323) does not match the PDE lattice (0.086); count equality (15 vs 16) is not a meaningful specimen metric on an arbitrary 42-cell domain. Vendor a pelage-pattern image (or digitise Sick et al. 2006 Fig. 2A/2C placode centroids), compare the NN-spacing distributions scale-free (spacing/lambda, CV, KS statistic) as a first-class measurement with a pass band, and state the vibrissa image as a secondary control.
- 2002-sick-wnt-dkk-hair-follicle.py:771-925 (peg ODE at 847, stage thresholds at 888-891) — the dermal coupling is one-way and the hair peg is a scalar depth with ordinal stage cut-offs at 25%/50% of a 12-layer slab. Replace the scalar with an epidermal invagination field (phase-field or level-set interface driven by the DC), add DC -> epidermis feedback (DC-derived Noggin/FGF maintaining the placode), and calibrate slab depth and peg length to micrometres from Paus et al. (1999) / Huh et al. (2013) sections so the stage histogram is testable rather than conventional.
- 2002-sick-wnt-dkk-hair-follicle.py:678-696 and 726-768 — all dermal constants (D_f, mu_f, s_f, D_b, mu_b, s_b, D_rho, chi_f, chi_b, v_peg) are hand-tuned dimensionless values with mechanism citations but no value provenance. Pin at least fibroblast speed / DC formation time to Glover et al. (2017) live-imaging data and the FGF20 range to Huh et al. (2013), record them in the JSON with units, and derive the dimensionless groups from them.
- 2002-sick-wnt-dkk-hair-follicle.py:836 and 943 — BMP4 is produced uniformly in proportion to rho (no interfollicular-epidermal or DC-localised source) and the BMP4 axis is evaluated at a single 5x point. Add a spatially structured BMP source (interfollicular epidermis as in Mou et al. 2006) and a bmp4_scale sweep showing monotone suppression of DC count / peg depth, with a Noggin rescue control (Botchkarev 1999).
- 2002-sick-wnt-dkk-hair-follicle.py:847, 860 and 867-876 — e_thr = 1.6 is used both as the peg-ODE onset (847) and as the DC segmentation threshold (860), and the DC-placode registration radius 2.5 px is arbitrary; the DC/placode ratio 1.0 and Fgf20-null = 0 are therefore partly by construction. Decouple detection (e.g. Otsu on the upper-dermal density) from the dynamics threshold and report sensitivity of N_DC and offset to both.
- docs/media/wnt_dkk_hair_follicle_comparison.png row D — no reference image (Huh 2013 Fig. 1 DC sections, Glover 2017 live-imaging aggregation, or a Paus 1999 stage panel) is shown beside the model, so the condensate shape, depth (centre of mass 0.41 layers) and peg morphology are judged against nothing; vendor a CC-licensed DC/hair-germ section and segment it as was done for Panel C1.
