## Findings and remediation

### Hamant et al. (2008), Bozorg et al. (2014), Sampathkumar et al. (2014), Uyttewaal et al. (2012), Heisler et al. (2010) & Louveaux et al. (2016) shoot apical meristem shell stress tensor, stress-vs-strain CMT alignment, katanin boundary morphogenesis and PIN1/CMT co-alignment on a growing L1 cellular vertex mesh  —  overall 71.0 (mostly faithful)

Pass 3 (v3) evaluation of `2008-hamant-microtubule-stress-meristem.py` (overall weighted score 71.0/100, down from 93.4 in v2). The remediation adds a genuine mechanistic layer: a growing 2D L1 cellular vertex mesh (`L1VertexTissue`, L509-985; `simulate_l1_vertex_patch`, L1020-1156) in which the zone shell stress tensor is applied as boundary traction, Hookean wall tensions feed a per-cell virial stress tensor, CMT directors follow it with katanin gain, cellulose reinforcement closes the feedback loop, PIN1 is allocated to the most stressed walls and transports auxin, cells divide along sigma_1 and T1 flips are detected. It reproduces, emergently, Heisler (2010)'s co-alignment of PIN1-rich walls with CMTs (cos^2 0.747 WT vs 0.47 oryzalin), PIN1 polarity away from an ablated cell in WT and microtubule-independently under oryzalin (0.83 / 0.83), circumferential CMTs around the wound (0.985), a monotone katanin sweep (r = 0.995) and Louveaux (2016)'s stress-vs-Errera contrast (1.00 vs 0.18), with the Euler characteristic preserved over 420 divisions and two new machine-checked Lean theorems. The re-score is lower because the Pass 2 layers that v2 credited as 'FEM' and 'measurements' are prescribed constants and closed-form fits whose headline correlations compare the reference JSON with itself, the mesh is 2D rather than the requested 3D, it does not reproduce the Bozorg discrimination, and it over-predicts dome CMT order.

Remediation:
- 2008-hamant-microtubule-stress-meristem.py:1397-1405 with 412-432 — the 'Bozorg stress cue vs strain-cue correlation' measurement reports emp_r, a literal 0.991 / -0.415 copied from the reference JSON, and the 5-condition sweep's S_pred / depth / cos^2 are closed-form fits (strain-cue and oryzalin rows are pure constants). Replace both with values computed on the vertex mesh (e.g. correlation between the mesh CMT director field and the shell sigma_1 field under stress vs strain cue; crease depth from an out-of-plane buckling or curvature proxy) or delete the measurements and the r = 0.9992 panel title.
- 2008-hamant-microtubule-stress-meristem.py:509-606 and 1046-1047 — the mesh is planar; the saddle's K < 0 enters only as boundary pre-strain (76:12 MPa -> 0.19:0.03 strain). Embed the vertex positions on a parametrised surface (sphere cap for the dome, hyperbolic paraboloid for the boundary, cylinder for the stem), apply turgor as a normal load and recover the in-plane stress from the shell equilibrium so that Law 1's Gaussian-curvature inversion is tested on the mesh rather than imposed.
- 2008-hamant-microtubule-stress-meristem.py:1137-1155 and 1451-1478 — the Bozorg stress-vs-strain discrimination does not emerge: strain-cue ablation gives circumferential CMTs (0.968) like the stress cue (0.985), and the saddle strain-cue S (0.49) barely differs from WT (0.57). Implement growth-strain (not elastic wall strain) as the strain cue — the rate tensor of rest-length creep per cell — which is what Bozorg et al. show becomes perpendicular to the stiffened direction under feedback.
- 2008-hamant-microtubule-stress-meristem.py:1206-1212 and Panel I — dome WT S = 0.42 vs reference 0.082 (zone RMSE 0.24): growth-induced cell-scale stress anisotropy (0.31) under an isotropic boundary drives CMT order the dome should not have. Add a cell-scale averaging length or an anisotropy threshold below which S collapses (Hamant 2008 reports isotropic CMTs in the CZ), and sweep it so the dome / saddle contrast becomes a tested prediction.
- 2008-hamant-microtubule-stress-meristem.py:288-355 and Panel A title — `build_2d_meristem_fem_field` is a sum of Gaussians; rename it (`build_2d_meristem_anisotropy_sketch`) and retitle Panel A, or replace it with a real 2D membrane solve on the cross-section profile.
- 2008-hamant-microtubule-stress-meristem.py:1438 and 1447 — specimen coherency and area-fraction measurements use reference_value = value. Either digitise a published coherency value (e.g. Sampathkumar 2014 Fig. 2 anisotropy) or mark reference_value null and let measure_outputs report 'no reference recorded'.
- datasets/microtubule_stress_meristem/pin1_cmt_coalignment_reference.json:70-118 — figure numbers for Heisler 2010 and Louveaux 2016 are unverified and the thresholds are ordinal. Verify against the PDFs and digitise at least one quantitative distribution (Heisler 2010 PIN1-vs-CMT angle histogram; Louveaux 2016 division-angle vs stress histogram) so the co-alignment and division measurements get numeric references.
- 2008-hamant-microtubule-stress-meristem.py:519-530 and 1993-2032 — expose E_shell, k_wall, K_A, beta_pin, kappa_cmt and phi_wall on the CLI, add a `--sweep PARAM:lo:hi:n` over them with a rendered panel, and add a `--seed` sweep so the self-test thresholds (0.70, 0.66) are shown to hold across seeds rather than sitting just below one seed's values.
