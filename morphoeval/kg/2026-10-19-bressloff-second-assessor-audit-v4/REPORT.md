Blinded second-assessor pass. The script implements the Bressloff et al. (2001/2002) V1 orientation-hypercolumn model with both the upstream perturbative dispersion/bare-overlap path (reproducing all 5 stability reports and 24 preset runs in paper-calibration.json to 2.22e-16) and an exact orientation-operator diagonalization with full resolvent-coupled cubic amplitude equations (2001 Eqs. 5.9-5.15), seamless conformal complex-log retinal pullbacks, orientation line elements via the tangent map, a paper-scale tunnel preset, 1D/2D parameter sweeps, and Horton & Hoyt (1991) cortical-magnification anchors.
