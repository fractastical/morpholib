Remediation:
- 1976-cooke-clock-and-wavefront.py:136-148 and 266-336 — the FGF/RA toggle is a two-variable reduction with tuned normalised constants (alpha = 3, K = 1, n = 4, tau_FR = 12 min, k_dM = 0.0074/min) and Cyp26/Raldh2 lumped into two Hill terms. Implement the published Goldbeter et al. (2007) kinetics with explicit Cyp26 and Raldh2 and their parameter values, vendor the parameter table, and reproduce the paper's hysteresis figure quantitatively (bistable window position and width) instead of only locating M_lo = 0.53.
- 1976-cooke-clock-and-wavefront.py:765-876 — the vertex sheet has no T1 transitions, so the lattice zig-zag cleft can only rotate from 28.2 to 10.3 deg and its tortuosity floors at 1.03; there is no apical constriction or rosette term, so 'epithelial rosette cleft rotation' is reduced to cleft-edge reorientation. Add T1 edge swaps (edge length threshold) and a per-somite apical-constriction / rosette energy, and model the 90-degree Xenopus cell-block rotation (Afonin et al. 2006) as a measurable rotation of cell long axes.
- 1976-cooke-clock-and-wavefront.py:883-976 and datasets/clock_and_wavefront/clock_and_wavefront_reference.json:30-41 — the SEM specimen is a chick embryo in pixels without a scale bar, so S_obs = 48.47 px and the 7.9-deg cleft angle cannot be compared to S_sim in um or to Xenopus somites. Vendor a scale-calibrated Xenopus laevis somite micrograph (or add a scale for the chick SEM) and compare S_obs in um to S_sim.
- 1976-cooke-clock-and-wavefront.py:125-132 — EXPECTED_SHA256 pins only the specimen and scout manifest; fgf_ra_vertex_reference.json and clock_and_wavefront_reference.json are recorded at runtime but not pinned, so the reference values used by measure_morphoeval() can drift silently. Pin both digests.
- 1976-cooke-clock-and-wavefront.py:1141-1149 — the FGF8 bead measurement is compared to a control ratio of 1.0 with a directional reference because Dubrulle et al. (2001) report no numerical somite-size ratio. Digitise the bead-side vs control somite sizes from Dubrulle (2001) or the simulated ratio from Goldbeter (2007) and use that as reference_value.
- 1976-cooke-clock-and-wavefront.py:150-161 and 800-825 — dLambda_cleft = 0.9 is tested only against 0, and the relaxation schedule (150 gradient steps per 40-min snapshot) has no physical time scale. Sweep dLambda over [0, 1.5] and show cleft angle and tortuosity are monotone, and calibrate the step/viscosity to a measured boundary-straightening time.
- 1976-cooke-clock-and-wavefront.py:944-958 — the empirical cleft angle is a luminance-minimum line fit through 4-px bands over a 44-px row for 8 clefts, without uncertainty. Replace by an edge-traced boundary (e.g. Canny + Hough on the inter-somitic furrow) and report mean +/- sd for both rows.
