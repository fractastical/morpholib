### Sheth et al. (2012) Science distal Hoxa13/Hoxd11-13 + Gli3 dose modulation of Turing digit wavelength on a fan-shaped embryonic autopod, with Pass 3 AER-FGF8 mitogenic outgrowth on a deforming Lagrangian mesh  —  overall 91.3 (faithful)

Pass 3 (v3) post-remediation evaluation of `2012-sheth-hox-turing-polydactyly.py` (overall weighted score: 91.3 / 100, -0.8 from 92.1 in Pass 2). The single open Pass 2 item is closed as an honest reduced version: the 2D polar fan Turing solver is now coupled to a deforming Lagrangian mesh whose distal rim grows under an AER-FGF8 mitogen field with prescribed kinematic dilation, solved in the Crampin-Gaffney-Maini ALE form (dilution, mesh-relative advection, deformed polar metric, backward-Euler radial diffusion). The growing fan reproduces monotone digit addition with Hox loss (N_grow = 3.5, 5.5, 7.5, 13.0 for H = 1, 7/9, 5/9, 0), monotone digit number with AER-driven width over a g_max sweep (r = 0.996 against N = L/lambda, slope 0.90), the Summerbell AER-ablation ordering (L = 1365, 1667, 1990, 2500 um), and the growth-dilution threshold sigma_max - g_rim = 2.54 > 0 (Lean Theorem 5). The remediation also exposed that the Pass 2 solvers seed their target mode and that the WT growing fan under-counts digits (3.5 vs 5) through a growth-induced wavelength lag; model-fidelity, specimen-match and morphometric-validation are scored down accordingly while traceability and reproducibility rise.

Remediation:
- 2012-sheth-hox-turing-polydactyly.py:512-703 and 631 — growth is kinematic (prescribed dilation, div v = g) and digit-ray orientation is imposed by eps_r = 0.05 radial/angular diffusion anisotropy. Replace with a mechanical tissue model (e.g. Stokes/viscous or Morishita-Iwasa elastic growth with an FGF8-driven volumetric source and stress-dependent anisotropy) and derive stripe orientation from a Raspopovic et al. (2014) Fgf/Wnt proximodistal modulation of the kinetics instead of the anisotropy.
- 2012-sheth-hox-turing-polydactyly.py:284 and 375-376 — the 1D arc and the fixed-width fan seed the target cosine mode (-0.08 cos(n_target s), -0.10 cos(k_c s)), so the Pass 2 "exact integer mode lock" [5, 7, 8, 9, 11, 14] and the 2 -> 5 / 2 -> 14 fan counts are partly initial-condition artefacts (the noise-only growing fan gives 3.5 WT / 13.0 null). Report noise-only counts for all solvers (with an ensemble over seeds) and move the seeded runs to an explicitly labelled 'mode-locked' control.
- 2012-sheth-hox-turing-polydactyly.py:179-183 — k_c(H) = 14 - 9H and g_u(H) = 200 f_u + 25 k_c^4 - 4.5 |tr J_kc| are closure ansaetze that pin sigma_max ~ 4.5; digitise Sheth et al.'s supplementary parameter table and Fig. 2G digit-period vs autopod-width measurements into datasets/hox_turing_polydactyly/ and fit H -> k_c and the dispersion against them.
- 2012-sheth-hox-turing-polydactyly.py:431-445 — GROWTH_R0 = 1.6, GROWTH_L_F = 0.5, GROWTH_T_AER_OFF = 2.4 and GROWTH_T_TOTAL = 5.0 are unsourced modelling choices in model time; anchor them to Boehm et al. (2010) limb-bud dimensions / proliferation rates and convert model time to hours (E10.5-E12.5), so sigma_max and g_rim become comparable with measured cell-cycle times.
- 2012-sheth-hox-turing-polydactyly.py:706-730 and 1147-1179 — the width-law slope 0.90 (Panel L) mixes growth-rate and final-width effects because the g_max sweep changes both; add a sweep at fixed final L (vary g_max and T jointly) to isolate the Crampin growth lag and test peak insertion explicitly.
- 2012-sheth-hox-turing-polydactyly.py:1364-1376 — the AER-ablation measurement is referenced only to the intact control (1.0) with Summerbell (1974) cited qualitatively; digitise Summerbell's stage-vs-truncation table so the ablation series has quantitative reference values.
