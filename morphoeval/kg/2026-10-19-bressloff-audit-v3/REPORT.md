## Findings and remediation

### Bressloff et al. (2001/2002) V1 Euclidean Symmetry-Breaking & Klüver Form Constants  —  overall 67.7 (mostly faithful)

The script implements the Bressloff et al. (2001/2002) orientation-hypercolumn model in recognisable form: the exact two-regime retino-cortical map with the paper's w0 and eps and an analytic inverse, Fourier-Bessel lateral harmonics with the sin(2n theta0)/(2n theta0) spread factor, even/odd perturbative dispersion curves to O(beta^2), cubic normal-form stability tests (roll stable iff 2 gamma3(theta) > gamma0) and the tangent map phi_R = phi + theta_R for line elements. Its numerical parity is with the vendored MesmerPrism Rust port (max |diff| 2.2e-16 on 5 stability_reports and 24 runs), which is stated plainly; the fixture's 12 failed paper- expectation checks, the alpha/lambda_c discrepancies and the Rule (2011) calibration_claim_allowed=false status are disclosed in the ledger, CSV and self-test rather than hidden. The remaining substantive gaps are that the cubic coefficients are bare eigenfunction overlap integrals, contoured vs non-contoured is a preset flag rather than a derived W0/W1 criterion, no swept model parameter changes the selected lattice family (rhombic everywhere), the rendered wavelength is 3.1x the paper's perceptual estimate, and the full-field spiral carries a seam spanning most of the 40 deg disc. Rendering is byte-identical across runs and pinned to a lock file.

- **Model fidelity to source: 70** — The map x = (alpha/eps) ln(1 + eps r/w0), y = beta r theta/(w0 + eps r) with w0 = 0.087, eps = 0.051 is the paper's, and its inverse, the lateral Fourier-Bessel harmonics exp(-xi^2 q^2/4) I_n(xi^2 q^2/4) with (-1)^n sign and the theta0 spread factor, the O(beta) + O(beta^2) even/odd dispersion relation and the 2 gamma3(theta) > gamma0 roll criterion all match the 2001 formulation as I know it. Deductions: alpha = 3/pi and beta = 1.589/2 are the Rust port's values, not the paper's (foveal magnification 10.98 vs the stated 11.5 mm/deg), kept for fixture parity (-4); gamma3(theta) and eta are reduced to the bare overlap integrals of u(phi) with unit gain, omitting the firing-rate derivative weights and quadratic-feedback terms of Eq. 5.15, so the 'rolls and squares never stable' verdict that drives every SELECTED/IMPOSED badge rests on a reduced functional (-8); contoured vs non-contoured is a preset flag, whereas the paper derives it from whether the n = 0 or n = 1 local harmonic is critical, and branch_growth always takes W1 as its base (-5); the lateral widths xi = 1, xi_hat = 1.5 hypercolumns are upstream choices that put lambda_c at 8.7 mm, 3.1x the paper's 2.4-3.2 mm, and the q scan is capped at 3.5 so the paper's scale is unreachable (-5); the default full-field theta in (-pi, pi] convention departs from the paper's hemispheric map and the resulting seam is understated as 'near the fovea' (-4); tanh sharpness, the m <= 4 eigenfunction truncation and the +/-1.5 clamp are upstream artifacts, not paper steps, and quintic selection is absent (-4).
