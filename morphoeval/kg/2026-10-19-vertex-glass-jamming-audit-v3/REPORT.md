### Bi et al. (2015), Park et al. (2015) & Bi et al. (2016) zero-temperature shape-index unjamming, asthmatic bronchial epithelial rigidity transition and self-propelled Voronoi motility-driven glass transition  —  overall 90.1 (faithful)

Pass 3 (v3) evaluation of `2015-bi-vertex-glass-jamming.py` (90.1/100, down from 91.0 in Pass 2). The single open v2 item is closed: the script now implements the Bi et al. (2016) Self-Propelled Voronoi model with exact analytic forces on Voronoi centres (verified against finite differences to a relative error of 9e-9, momentum-conserving, margin-tiling identical to the full 3x3 tiling), an active Brownian integrator, FIRE quench, and finite-time dynamical (D_eff, T1 rate) and structural (<q>) order parameters. It maps the (p_0, v_0) glass boundary at D_r = 1, which is monotonically lowered by motility (alpha = 0.575) and extrapolates to p_0^c(0) = 3.850 against Bi's 3.81; the boundary shape index is q_c = 3.818 +/- 0.013 against the Bi (2016) Fig. 4 value 3.81; the D_r slice shows the solid region growing with D_r (p_0^c 3.677 -> 3.748) and D_s falling; and the energy-minimised v_0 = 0 <q>(p_0) reproduces the Bi (2015) pinning curve with r = 0.9966. The overall score nonetheless falls because the force-based module exposes that the original regime simulator, p_0 sweep and bronchial trajectories are phenomenological (phase-tabulated stretch, noise, migration speed; curve-fit q(t)), the T1-barrier/shear law is hard-coded, the boundary criterion is script-defined and finite-time with one seed, and one new reference number is an unverified figure reading. Backed by 6 machine-checked Lean 4 theorems (0 sorrys) and 13 self-test checks.
