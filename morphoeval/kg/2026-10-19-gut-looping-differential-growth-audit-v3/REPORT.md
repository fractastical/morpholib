Pass 3 (v3) evaluation of `2011-savin-gut-looping-differential-growth.py` (overall weighted score 80.0/100, down from 92.6 in v2). The single v2 remediation item is closed honestly: the gut rod now shares its nodes with a 760-triangle St.Venant-Kirchhoff membrane mesh of the fan-shaped dorsal mesentery with a mean-preserving apicobasal Pitx2 stiffness field, and loop number, radius and tortuosity emerge from L-BFGS-B minimisation (analytic gradient verified to 8.2e-11 against finite differences); the prescribed-shape target and calibration divisors that produced the v2 near-perfect matches were removed.
