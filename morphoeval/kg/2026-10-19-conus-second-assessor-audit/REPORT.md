## Findings and remediation

### Wolfram (1984) Rule 30 & Meinhardt–Klingler (1987) Conus textile Pigmentation  —  overall 50.5 (partially faithful)

Blinded second-assessor pass. The script evolves Wolfram's elementary CA Rule 30 (plus a Rule 90 control) on a periodic ring, verifies the centre column against OEIS A051023, checks Jen's left-bijective inverse, the left-cone diagonal recursion and period-doubling lock-in cascade, and a Boolean-Jacobian damage cone. In parallel it integrates a three-variable activator/substrate/relaxation PDE labelled Meinhardt-Klingler (1987) with an added stochastic activator-injection rule, texture-maps both space-time sheets onto a single fixed Raup-style biconical mesh, and compares silhouette H/W, IoU, white-chord statistics and a box-counting dimension against numbers vendored in a SHA-256-pinned fixture derived (by unpublished means) from two Wikimedia Conus textile photographs. A second figure adds unrelated cell-sorting and bioelectric domain-wall kymographs. I re-ran the script (35 s) and obtained byte-identical CSV, PNG and GIF outputs.

- **Model fidelity to source: 62** — I verified by hand that the Boolean form l XOR (c OR r) and the GF(2) ANF reproduce the Rule 30 truth table (00011110), that run_eca uses the standard Wolfram neighbourhood indexing so Rule 90 is also correct, that Jen's inverse a_{t,i-1} = a_{t+1,i} XOR (a_{t,i} OR a_{t,i+1}) follows algebraically and is applied with the right time offsets, that the diagonal recursion d_k(t+1) = d_{k-2}(t) XOR (d_{k-1}(t) OR d_k(t)) is an exact change of variables, and that the three Boolean partial derivatives are right; the CA half is exemplary. The Meinhardt-Klingler half is a loose analogue, not the published equations: autocatalysis is divided by a relaxation variable b with db/dt = mu_b (a - b), which is neither the 1987 activator-depleted-substrate system nor the hormone that modulates activator decay in the Oliva model, parameter names and values are unsourced, and a stochastic rule injecting +0.90 activator at random high-substrate sites plus hard clipping of a, s, b is load-bearing (with the injection removed the pattern extinguishes to 0.9 percent pigment in steps 148-221 and a saturates at the 5.0 clip); deduction 20. The Raup mesh uses W^(theta/2pi) and r_c = a(1+D)/(1-D) correctly but replaces Raup's generating curve with an ad hoc envelope carrying six unsourced constants, so that T has no effect on the silhouette for T in [0, 0.36]; deduction 10. Remaining 8 points lost for over-naming a sufficiency check as an iff theorem, a 0.36 vs 0.40 D2 mismatch in the bioelectric panel, and cross-model panels that are toy re-implementations.
