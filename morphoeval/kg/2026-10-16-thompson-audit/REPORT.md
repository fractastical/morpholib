## Findings and remediation

### Thompson's Scarus -> Pomacanthus coordinate transformation fitted to real species  —  overall 70.2 (mostly faithful)

Loads and hash-checks the 791-species x 17-landmark table; generalised Procrustes superimposition (unit centroid size, no reflection), rotated so the snout-to-peduncle axis is horizontal; genus means. From the Scarus mean to the Pomacanthus mean fits five maps - similarity (Procrustes distance 0.0312), affine (residual 0.0259, 31 % of the squared distance explained), Moebius z -> (az+b)/(cz+1) (0.0311, 0.8 %; Thompson's "system of coaxial circles" read as isogonal), affine then Moebius (0.0256, 32 %; oblique coaxial circles as Fig. 376 is drawn) and the thin-plate spline (exact, bending energy 0.678) - after checking that the two non-linear fitters recover known maps to 1e-17. Reports the within-Pomacanthus RMS (0.0132, species 0.0090-0.0180) and within-Scarus RMS (0.0099) as the noise floor, a leave-one-landmark-out RMS for the oblique family (0.0371, above the 0.0312 of no deformation), the depth/length change (0.377 -> 0.587; affine singular-value ratio 1.27), the per-landmark residuals after the affine fit (largest LM7, LM2, LM4, LM8), the same fits for all 3782 ordered pairs of 62 genus means (median explained by oblique coaxial circles 43 %; this pair at the 31st percentile) and Scarus -> each Chaetodon species (12-61 %, median 35 %). Figure: Thompson's Figs. 375-376 cropped from the hash-pinned scan beside the superimposed real species with numbered landmarks; five grid panels carrying the Scarus mean and its grid under each map with residual vectors to the real Pomacanthus mean; the residual ladder against the noise floors; the context scatter. GIF: the best grid of Thompson's family and the thin-plate spline grid morphing side by side.

- **Model fidelity to source: 72** — Thompson published no equations, so fidelity is to his words and figure. "Coaxial circles" is formalised as the Moebius group, the only maps that carry straight lines to circles, and the script states that reading in the docstring; because Fig. 376's two pencils meet obliquely, a second family (affine then Moebius) is fitted so that the figure as drawn is covered and not only its stated idealisation. The alternative Thompson compares against ("rectilinear co-ordinates", i.e. the identity/similarity) and the one he does not (affine) are fitted the same way, and the exact deformation is the thin-plate spline of Bookstein 1989, the standard successor to the method. The fitters are checked on synthetic maps before the real fit. Not higher because Thompson's own drawn grid is not digitised and compared with the fitted one (the comparison is grid-family to data, not his constants to the fitted constants); because his "typical Scaroid fish" and unnamed Pomacanthus are replaced by genus Procrustes means, which is the right test of the general claim but not of his drawing; and because the fish are 17 landmarks, not the outline Thompson deformed, so curvature between landmarks is untested.

Remediation:
- Digitise Thompson's drawn grid of Fig. 376 from the pinned scan, fit the Moebius constants to it, and compare them with the constants fitted to the species, so his drawing is tested and not only his family.
- Add outlines (semilandmarks along the body profile, or the Bishop Museum photographs of one Scarus and one Pomacanthus) so the comparison is of the form Thompson deformed, not 17 points.
- Sweep the complex constant c at fixed affine part in a panel, and report the fitted constants for the 54 Chaetodon species to test "small variations in the values of the constants" directly.
- Estimate digitising error (replicate landmarking of a few photographs) so the noise floor can be separated into species and measurement variance.
- Pin the Python 3.14 environment (exact versions) in requirements or a lock file.
