## Findings and remediation

### Bressloff et al. (2001/2002) V1 Euclidean Symmetry-Breaking & Klüver Form Constants  —  overall 53.0 (partially faithful)

The script is a careful, fully deterministic Python port of the MesmerPrism/bressloff-v1-form-constants Rust formalism: Fourier-Bessel lateral harmonics with the (-1)^n sign and sin(2n theta0)/(2n theta0) anisotropy factor, Gaussian-difference local harmonics, the second-order perturbative dispersion relation, cubic gamma_3(theta) coefficients and the 2*gamma_3(theta) > gamma_0 roll-stability test all have the published structure, and the PNG, CSV and GIF regenerate byte-for-byte from SHA-pinned fixtures. Its headline "exact parity to 1e-16" is parity with another implementation's reported numbers, not with Bressloff et al.; the vendored fixture itself records that the Fig. 40 odd-hex case selects "honeycomb" where the paper expects "triangle", and the script prints [PASS] for it. The rendered form constants are chosen by preset (pattern, wave_count) rather than by the amplitude equations or the critical wavenumber, the retino-cortical map is the far-field asymptote rather than Eq. 2.1, and the Rule (2011) panel plots upstream Floquet boundaries whose own fit report says calibration_claim_allowed=false. Tunnel and spiral pullbacks look like Klüver/Bressloff figures; the honeycomb is a dot lattice, the GIF's cobweb/honeycomb frames are unrecognisable, and no morphometric is compared against any empirical or literature value.

- **Model fidelity to source: 55** — The lateral coupling W_n^lat(q) = (-1)^n/2 [e^{-x} I_n(x) - I e^{-x'} I_n(x')] with x = xi^2 q^2/4 and the sin(2n theta0)/(2n theta0) spread factor, the local Gaussian-difference harmonics e^{-2n^2 xi^2}, the second-order dispersion relation lambda_pm = W_1 + beta(W0 +- W2) + beta^2 sum (...)^2/(W_1 - W_m), and the roll/lattice criterion 2 gamma_3(theta) > gamma_0 all match the 2001 paper as I know it, and the Fig. 38 numbers (gamma_0 = 0.3768, gamma_3(2pi/3) = 0.1869) agree with the Lean Section 14 instance. -12: the "exact parity" is against the Rust implementation's actual outputs, and the fixture's own fig40_odd_hex_bifurcation check (expected triangle, actual honeycomb, passed=false) is silently reported as [PASS] because the script compares only to rep["selected_*"]. -8: branch_selection_for scopes selection to the preset's lattice, so identical gamma/q* produce "hexagonal" for fig38 and "rhombic" for rhombic_stability_angle; the selected family is an input, not a model prediction. -7: for every non-auto preset the rendered wavenumber is 2 pi wave_count / extent rather than the critical q*, so the cortical wavelength never comes from the marginal-stability curve. -6: the pullback uses the asymptotic r_R = (w0/eps) exp(eps x/alpha) instead of Eq. 2.1's x = (alpha/eps) ln(1 + eps r/w0), y = beta r theta/(w0 + eps r), which introduces an artificial foveal singularity that has to be masked. -6: ad-hoc regularisers not in the paper (coefficient clips to +-1.5, safe_denominator, a max-over-phi scalar field standing in for the oriented-segment double map). -6: the m = 0 normalisation of the second-order term and the alpha = 3/pi, beta = 1.589/2 constants are taken from the Rust code and cannot be checked against a cited equation.
