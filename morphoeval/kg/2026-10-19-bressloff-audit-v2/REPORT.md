## Findings and remediation

### Bressloff et al. (2001/2002) V1 Euclidean Symmetry-Breaking & Klüver Form Constants  —  overall 62.8 (mostly faithful)

The script is a careful Python port of the MesmerPrism/karacsm Rust implementation of the Bressloff et al. (2001/2002) orientation-hypercolumn model: the local and lateral Fourier-Bessel coupling harmonics, the second-order perturbative dispersion relation for the even/odd branches, the cubic amplitude coefficients gamma_3(theta)/eta_hex and the roll-stability criterion 2*gamma_3(theta) > gamma_0 all have the published form, and the exact two-regime retino-cortical map is implemented and inverted analytically. The headline "parity to 2e-16" is parity with the Rust fixture, not with any number in the paper, and the four Klüver gallery panels are imposed lattices (rings/spiral/cobweb) whose cubic amplitude equations actually select hexagons; this is printed under each panel but the gallery is still presented as the model's form constants. The only genuinely empirical comparison is the Horton & Hoyt (1991) magnification law (6.8% RMS, with the A/E2 mismatch disclosed); the output morphometrics are self-consistency checks against the model's own q*, and the Rule (2011) panel is a pass-through of an upstream fixture that fails its own acceptance. Provenance (DOIs, PMIDs, upstream commit, SHA-256 pins, lock file, deterministic self-test) is strong, but the fixture-failure ledger reports only the 11 stability_reports checks and omits the 11 additional failures in the fixture's runs[] checks.

- **Model fidelity to source: 72** — The core equations are implemented in the published form: W_n = exp(-2 n^2 xi^2) - A exp(-2 n^2 xi_hat^2) for the local coupling, the lateral harmonics as the Hankel transform of a normalised Gaussian difference exp(-xi^2 q^2/4) I_n(xi^2 q^2/4) with the anisotropy factor sin(2 n theta_0)/(2 n theta_0) and sign (-1)^n, the dispersion relation lambda_pm(q) = W_1 + beta (W_0 pm W_2) + beta^2 sum_m (W_{m-1} pm W_{m+1})^2/(W_1 - W_m), gamma_3(theta) and eta_hex as orientation integrals of the eigenfunction, the roll criterion 2 gamma_3(theta) > gamma_0, and the exact map x = (alpha/eps) ln(1 + eps r/w_0), y = beta r theta/(w_0 + eps r). Deductions: parity is verified only against the Rust port's own scan values, never against a number, curve or critical wavenumber reported in the paper (-8); branch selection adds a non-paper free-energy "score" ranking, a lattice-local fallback, coefficient clipping to +-1.5 and an assumed-stable even hexagon rather than the paper's per-lattice stability analysis (-6); the "contoured" planform is rendered as max over phi of a scalar activity then tanh-sharpened, discarding the orientation field that defines contoured planforms in Figs 31-36 (-6); the map constants alpha = 3/pi and beta = 1.589/2 are asserted without derivation (-3); the gallery presets silently inherit lateral_wide_sigma = 1.5 while the Fig 16/17 panel uses 3.0, so the rendered q* = 1.45 is not the q* = 1.0 shown in Panel A (-5).
