## Findings and remediation

### Bressloff et al. (2001/2002) V1 Euclidean Symmetry-Breaking & Klüver Form Constants  —  overall 78.2 (mostly faithful)

Blinded second-assessor pass. 2001-bressloff-v1-form-constants.py faithfully implements the Bressloff et al. (2001/2002) retino-cortical log-polar mapping, intra-hypercolumn Fourier harmonics, anisotropic lateral Fourier-Bessel coupling, second-order perturbative dispersion relations, and cubic amplitude-equation integrals, matching the upstream Rust reference report on five stability targets to 2.22e-16. However, several headline claims exceed what the Python code actually computes: Rule et al. (2011) flicker-phosphene Floquet tongues are not simulated at all but loaded from a pre-computed JSON fixture whose own metadata flags it as failing calibration acceptance thresholds. Furthermore, odd-parity hexagonal versus triangular planform selection omits the fifth-order amplitude terms required when the quadratic coefficient vanishes, cortical planforms are synthesized via a scalar max-over-orientation tanh surrogate rather than Bressloff's double-map oriented contours, and all quantitative validation compares against another software implementation rather than empirical psychophysical or anatomical measurements.

- **Model fidelity to source: 75** — The script accurately implements the log-polar retino-cortical pullback parameters (epsilon=0.051, w0=0.087, alpha=3/pi, beta=1.589/2) from Bressloff et al. (2001 Section 2; 2002 Eq. 2.1), the local orientation Fourier coefficients W_n (Eq. 3.12), the lateral Fourier-Bessel harmonics W_n^lat(q) (Eq. 4.9), the second-order perturbative eigenvalue branches lambda_even/odd(q) (Section 4), and the quadratic and cubic amplitude-equation integrals gamma_2 and gamma_3(theta) (Eqs. 5.14-5.15). We deduct 10 points because despite claiming in the module docstring to implement Rule, Stoffregen & Ermentrout (2011) flicker-phosphene Floquet resonance tongues, the script contains no E-I neural-field dynamics or Floquet monodromy solver and instead loads pre-computed curve coordinates directly from datasets/bressloff/rule-2011-floquet.json. We deduct 6 points because odd-parity hexagonal branch selection (fig40_odd_hex_bifurcation) sets eta_hex = 0.0 and unconditionally defaults to honeycomb without implementing the fifth-order amplitude terms required by Bressloff et al. (2001 Section 5.2, Fig. 40) to select odd triangles, reproducing an unfixed upstream check failure. We deduct 6 points because cortical and retinal planforms are rendered via static cosine superpositions with ad-hoc phase-drift weights (0.35, -0.25, 0.5), clamped eigenfunction coefficients in [-1.5, 1.5], and a scalar max-over-orientation tanh envelope rather than solving the neural-field PDE or rendering Bressloff's double-map orientation contours (and make_gif disables orientation coupling entirely via noncontoured mode). Finally, we deduct 3 points because for non-auto gallery presets such as fig31_square_even, render_cortical_and_retinal_planform ignores branch_selection_for (where 2*gamma_square > gamma0 makes squares unstable and selects roll/spiral) and force-renders the unstable square lattice while labeling its panel mode=spiral.
