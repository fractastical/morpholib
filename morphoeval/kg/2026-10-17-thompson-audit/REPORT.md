### Thompson's Scarus -> Pomacanthus coordinate transformation fitted to real species  —  overall 76.6 (mostly faithful)

Landmark test as in the 2026-10-16 audit, unchanged in code and numbers: 791 species x 17 landmarks, hash-checked; generalised Procrustes superimposition; from the Scarus genus mean to the Pomacanthus genus mean the similarity (0.0312), affine (0.0259, 31 % explained), Moebius (0.0311, 0.8 %), oblique coaxial (0.0256, 32 %) and thin-plate spline (exact, bending energy 0.678) fits, with the within-genus noise floor (Pomacanthus 0.0132), leave-one-landmark-out (0.0371), depth/length (0.377 -> 0.587), all 3782 genus pairs (this pair at the 31st percentile) and Scarus -> 54 Chaetodon species (median 35 %). New: the real-outline test. Two Commons photographs are fetched and hash-checked; the Scarus ghobban specimen is segmented by its Lab distance from white, the living Pomacanthus semicirculatus by a marker watershed (Mahalanobis colour distance from the frame-sampled sand plus colour gradient; markers are the declared box's outside and the 1.5 % least sand-like pixels plus the most textured pixels in their convex hull; shadow cleared below 0.745 of the height). Each silhouette is registered to its species' 17 landmarks by a similarity through two anchors the silhouette gives (snout -> LM2, caudal peduncle -> LM5-LM6 midpoint; the peduncle is tracked from the tail tip as the first local minimum of thickness between caudal fin and body). The five maps are fitted to the S. ghobban -> P. semicirculatus landmarks and applied to the Scarus silhouette; against the Pomacanthus silhouette the IoU is similarity 0.662, affine 0.652, Moebius 0.645, oblique coaxial 0.618, thin-plate spline 0.629, and the mean symmetric boundary distance 5.6, 6.5, 5.9, 7.0 and 6.6 % of body length. Second figure: the two photographs with silhouette, placed landmarks and anchors; both silhouettes in the Procrustes frame; five panels of the carried Scarus outline over the real Pomacanthus outline (with the coaxial and TPS grids); a bar chart of IoU and boundary distance with the similarity baseline marked; the caveats in the figure. --no-photos skips it.

Remediation:
- Digitise Thompson's drawn grid of Fig. 376 from the pinned scan, fit the Moebius constants to it, and compare them with the constants fitted to the species, so his drawing is tested and not only his family.
- Fit the maps to the outlines themselves (sliding semilandmarks on the two silhouettes) as well as to the landmarks, so Thompson's family gets its best chance on the form he deformed.
- Add more individuals per species (several Commons photographs each, or the Bishop Museum photographs the landmarks were digitised on) so the outline IoU has a spread, and prefer specimens in the same posture (both collected, or both living).
- Sweep the complex constant c at fixed affine part in a panel, and report the fitted constants for the 54 Chaetodon species to test "small variations in the values of the constants" directly.
- Estimate digitising error (replicate landmarking of a few photographs) so the noise floor can be separated into species and measurement variance.
- Pin the Python 3.14 environment (exact versions) in requirements or a lock file.
