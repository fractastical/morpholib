### Farhadifar et al. (2007) Drosophila wing disc apical vertex model, Lewis's law packing & PCP (Fz/Stbm) hinge-contraction vertex dynamics  —  overall 84.8 (faithful)

Pass 3 (v3) evaluation of `2007-farhadifar-drosophila-wing-vertex-model.py` (84.8/100, down from 90.9 in Pass 2). The single v2 remediation item is closed: Section 2b adds a genuine vertex model (tri-cellular vertices, analytic forces of Farhadifar Eq. 1 verified by finite differences, T1 exchanges) coupled to an explicit per-bond Frizzled/Strabismus asymmetry tensor with zero trace and perpendicular cancellation (Lean theorem 5), driven by a pupal hinge-contraction pure-shear strain with an Etournay-style shear decomposition (Lean theorem 6). At eps_pcp = 0.5 the model gives a cell-elongation peak <|Q|> = 0.253 (Etournay 2015 ~0.25, approx.), 20 T1s all on AP-oriented junctions versus 11 in the eps_pcp = 0 control, a rearrangement share of tissue shear of 0.307 (vs 0.165 control), bond-tension anisotropy Lambda_AP/Lambda_PD = 2.39 and a monotonic eps_pcp sweep. The re-score is lower because the audit now records what the v2 scores overlooked: the Section 2 regimes are Lloyd relaxations whose Lewis's-law fit is imposed by a post-hoc area reweighting, the only vendored specimen is a mouse epithelium, three new DOIs and all new reference values are unverified offline, and the PCP effect on hexagon fraction has the opposite sign to Classen et al. (2005).
