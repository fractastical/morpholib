## Findings and remediation

### Saadaoui et al. (2020), Serra et al. (2023) & Rozbicki et al. (2015) avian gastrulation tensile ring, Polonaise twin-vortex flow & primitive streak attractor  —  overall 67.3 (mostly faithful)

Pass 3 re-score of `2022-serra-gastrulation-streak-attractor.py` (67.3/100, down from 92.6 in Pass 2). Both Pass 2 remediation items were closed in code: an explicit Wnt8c -> Nodal -> FGF8 reaction-diffusion cascade with implicit spectral diffusion-decay and semi-Lagrangian advection now sources ring myosin through a pMLC activation field (`step_ligand_fields`, `myosin_phosphorylation_field`), with decay lengths verified against $\lambda = \sqrt{D/k}$ (FGF8 0.8 %, Nodal 1.4 %), and a reduced 2.5D apical-basal column model (`step_apical_basal_column`: apical constriction, basal expansion, constant-volume thickening $h = 2h_0/(a+b)$, basement-membrane decay, ingression rate $I$) feeds an irrotational sink $\nabla\cdot\mathbf{v} = -I$ into the planar flow (`solve_ingression_sink_velocity`). Perturbations are now receptor-level: SU5402 gives a uniform ring (asymmetry 0.00), SB431542 and Y-27632 give zero ingression, ectopic Vg1 gives a bipolar ring, and the emergent 5-regime peak speeds rank-correlate with Saadaoui/Serra at $\rho = 0.90$ under one WT-fixed calibration constant. Five Lean 4 theorems compile with 0 sorrys. The remediation, however, exposed that the v2 headline numbers (AP/ML 4.25, 2.40 um/min, $r = 1.0000$, SU5402 1.00) are prescribed lookups of the regime parameters, that about 82 % of the Polonaise flow is a hand-written streamfunction rather than the Stokes solution, and that the honest field-derived EMT zone is a posterior crescent (WT AP/ML 0.59 vs 4.25; 5-regime aspect $\rho = -0.20$) because mediolateral intercalation is absent. The code now discloses all of this (NOTE flags, `measured_*` keys, Panel I), which is credited; the scores reflect what the coupled model actually produces.
