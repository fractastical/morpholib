### Wiggs et al. (1997), Li et al. (2011) & Eskandari et al. (2015) cylindrical bilayer mucosal folding, period-doubling & Poiseuille airway resistance, coupled to a Weibel (1963) bronchial-tree impedance network with Venegas (2005) flow-stretch feedback  —  overall 84.3 (faithful)

Pass 3 (v3) evaluation of `2018-nelson-airway-mucosal-creasing.py` (overall weighted score 84.3/100; v2 was 93.4/100). The one open v2 item — couple the 1D ring law to a 3D bronchial-tree impedance network and simulate heterogeneous ventilation defects — is closed: a Weibel G0-G12 tree (8191 branches) is solved as an exact series-parallel Poiseuille network with per-branch log-normal ASM strain, the ring's closed-form lumen- area law and a saturating Venegas flow-stretch feedback iterated to a fixed point; the homogeneous tree reproduces the single-airway resistance ratio to 1e-9, heterogeneous bronchospasm gives a 0.350 ventilation- defect fraction for Severe Asthma (Venegas PET ~0.30) against 0.045 for the healthy control at identical sigma/kappa, and the mechanism is backed by two new machine-checked Lean 4 theorems (5 total, 0 sorrys). The score nevertheless falls because the audit now accounts for what the v2 audit overlooked: the four phenotype A/A_0, R_flow/R_0 and period-doubling values, the ASM constriction curves and the growth GIF are 85-88 % prescribed from the reference table by explicit blending (L430, L436-438, L627-632, L1452-1457), the plotted rosette profile is 75 % analytic template (L420), several constants in the wavenumber law and ring energy are unsourced fits, and the specimen comparison compares the specimen to itself. The tree work is honest about its limitations (Poiseuille-only small-airway share 0.43 vs 0.10-0.20; asthma/healthy tree amplification 2.80 vs a weak 3.66 reference built from a different single-airway source).
