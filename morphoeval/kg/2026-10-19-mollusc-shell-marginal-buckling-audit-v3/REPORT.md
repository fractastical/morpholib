- 2013-chirat-mollusc-shell-marginal-buckling.py:511-743 — the hollow-spine closure is a planar inextensible elastica with prescribed W = 6 mm, t = 0.35 mm and penalties mu = 400; no out-of-plane coupling between cross-sections, no shell stretching energy, no growth tensor. Read EI from the JSON (one stage), derive it from mantle thickness and modulus, and report the rib count as a prediction rather than a calibration target.
- 2013-chirat-mollusc-shell-marginal-buckling.py:1073-1139 — the 12-species quarter-power regression uses EI, k values that were set from lambda_obs, so r = 1.0000 and alpha = 0.2500 are tautological. - 2013-chirat-mollusc-shell-marginal-buckling.py:310, 357, 605, 2289-2300 — L-BFGS-B never meets ftol = 1e-16 / gtol = 1e-13 and always exits at maxiter (600; 500 in the trajectory), which is why self-test #10 needs a 1-2 % of A_eq tolerance. - 2013-chirat-mollusc-shell-marginal-buckling.py:234, 263, 328, 834-836 — the margin is periodic on [0, L) (FFT), so a repair-induced extra rib slides the whole 14 -> 15 pattern globally (adult correlation ~ 0 in defect cells of the phase diagram); the rib count also depends on a find_peaks prominence of 0.25 A_eq.
