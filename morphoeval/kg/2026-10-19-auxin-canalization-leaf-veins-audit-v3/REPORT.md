### Mitchison (1980, 1981) & Feugier, Mochizuki & Iwasa (2005) with-the-flux auxin canalization: exponent bifurcation, growing hex-lamina canalization (solved), Sachs wound bypass (solved + prescribed), hand-drawn Feugier venation templates, and cleared-leaf specimen segmentation  —  overall 70.1 (mostly faithful)

Pass 3 (v3) evaluation of `1997-mitchison-canalization-auxin-flux.py` (overall 70.1/100, down from 92.8 in v2). The remediation added a genuinely solved Mitchison (1980) canalization model on a growing hexagonal cell lattice (three cell-division stages, conductance inheritance, 16 measurements, 12 self-test checks, 3 new Lean 4 theorems) and a solved Sachs incision bypass with an alpha = 0 control. It reproduces the spatiotemporal order of vein formation in the weak sense that 77-81 % of newly canalized cells lie inside pre-existing intercostal panels and that vein order is stage-ordered, but it does NOT close loops (beta_1 = 0, specimen 1843 areoles), its solved network is a basal fan of lattice-aligned strands rather than a pinnate midrib with secondaries, its emergent S_vein/S_par (0.67) disagrees with the value v2 prescribed (0.31), and its strand angles (26.8 +- 14.2 deg) miss the specimen (52.9 deg). Building the solver exposed that the Pass 2 "simulations" of lamina drainage, wound bypass and Feugier topologies are analytic profiles, constants and hand-drawn graphs tuned to the script's own reference JSON; these are now labelled PRESCRIBED / GRAPH TEMPLATE in `measure_morphoeval()` but remain in the figure and the measurement set, and the v2 CLI flags are parsed but unused. Rendering is deterministic and SHA-pinned.
