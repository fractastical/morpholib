### Menshykau & Iber (2013) & Short et al. (2014) GDNF-RET-Wnt11-Sprouty1 kidney ureteric bud Turing branching morphogenesis  —  overall 88.1 (faithful)

Pass 3 (v3) evaluation of `2014-menshykau-gdnf-ret-ureteric-kidney.py` (overall weighted score 88.1/100, down from 92.8 in v2). The single v2 remediation item — couple the 3D tree to a phase-field / moving-boundary model of the expanding renal capsule and nephrogenic zone — is closed in a reduced, honestly labelled form: a steady diffuse-domain GDNF field on a fixed 36x36x39 grid inside an expanding spherical capsule with a nephrogenic-zone production shell and RET tip sinks, coupled to the tree by chemotaxis, moving-boundary confinement, interstitial dilation and GDNF-gated arrest. The coupling demonstrably matters: 67 % of E15.5 tips lie in the nephrogenic zone (decoupled v2 tree 11 %, with 80 % outside the capsule), a half-rate capsule suppresses the tip count to 198/293 through 103 vs 7 gated arrests, and the field is 2.15x enriched in the nephrogenic zone. Two Lean theorems (confinement bound, crowding-arrest monotonicity) are machine-checked, the self-test grows to 9 checks and 12 measurements, and three CLI flags plus the previously dead --D-ratio are wired. The lower score reflects what the remediation exposed rather than regressions: E15.5 tips 293 vs 354 observed (RMSE 23.3; v2's perfect fit came from a hard-coded WT branch_prob = 1.0 that is now removed), capsule constants that are order-of-magnitude anchors, a capsule radius prescribed from the observed tip counts, self-referential generation lengths/angles, a fragile Horton-Strahler R_b (2.18 at gate 0.15, 2.35 at 0.18), a specimen micrograph from a prorenin-receptor study with an unsourced pErk fold reference, and CLI overrides that do not reach the perturbation suite, sweep or capsule figure.
