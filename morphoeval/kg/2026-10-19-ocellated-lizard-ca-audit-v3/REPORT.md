### Manukyan et al. (2017) Nature hexagonal skin-scale border thickness reduction & outer-totalistic cellular automaton — 3D skin-volume reaction-diffusion (Pass 3)  —  overall 83.6 (faithful)

Pass 3 closes the single v2 remediation item in its honest reduced form: the script now integrates the reaction-diffusion system in a genuine 3D voxel skin volume with thinned inter-scale borders (`integrate_skin_volume_pde_3d`), compares it with its depth-averaged thin-film 2D reduction (90.3 % scale-state agreement, RMS 0.118, vertical non-uniformity 2.2e-2 at P = 0.17), and removes the artificial per-scale averaging that produced the v2 result. With the physics alone, the P = 0.17 skin volume discretises into bimodal scale states (inter/intra std ratio 14.7, Ashman D 4.88) versus 3.29 / 4.04 for continuum skin, insulated scales (P = 0) do not pattern, equal diffusion (d = 1) is quenched, and the P sweep is monotone (rho = -1). Linear stability analysis exposes that the vendored canonical parameter set is not Turing-unstable (d_c = 23.57 > 20). Overall weighted score 83.6 / 100, down from 91.9: the v2 scores credited the averaging hack, a leaky border band, un-provenanced histogram and parameter tables and a lattice-imposed segmentation; the remaining gaps (paper kinetics, micro-CT geometry and growth, scale-centre detection, CA rule derived from the PDE) are listed for Pass 4.
