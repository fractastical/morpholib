### Kondo & Asai (1995) Pomacanthus reaction-diffusion stripe insertion & Y-branch unzipping  —  overall 93.1 (faithful)

Pass 3 remediation re-score of `1995-kondo-pomacanthus-stripe-insertion.py` (93.1/100, up from 92.0 in Pass 2). Both v2 remediation items are closed with mechanism, not presentation: a curvilinear C-shaped polar-sector Kondo-Asai solver with the (1/rho) d/drho curvature term and a growing outer radius reproduces 3 -> 6 concentric arc insertion (initial radial spacing = lambda_c +/- 1.06 %, angular coherence 1.000 -> 0.9996, insertion at s* = 2.18 when the monotonically falling mid-trough inhibitor reaches 0.742 vs the proved zero-activator threshold 0.625), anchored on the specimen by a structure-tensor concentric-centre fit (8 arcs, radial spacing 2.62 mm vs the oblique row-wise 3.80 mm); and a discrete 128-cell iridophore/melanophore agent lattice coupled to the fields through bounded feedback reproduces the 6-stripe partition (fate concordance 0.93-0.97, agent stripes = field stripes for all kappa, +3.6..+11.7 % contrast gain). Two new Lean 4 theorems (5 total, 0 sorrys) formalise the threshold partition and the fate/feedback bounds. The next layer of defects is structural: the sector geometry is not fitted to the specimen outline, the effective insertion threshold is ~19 % above the proved bound, the agent layer is 1D/periodic with heuristic rule probabilities, and arc insertion still relies on injected background fluctuations.
