## Findings and remediation

### Mongera et al. (2018) & Kim et al. (2021) zebrafish tailbud active fluid-to-solid jamming transition & yield-stress axis elongation  —  overall 90.0 (faithful)

Pass 3 (v3) evaluation of `2022-tailbud-neuromesodermal-jamming.py` (90.0/100, down 1.4 from 91.4 in Pass 2). The one open v2 item is closed in a reduced, explicitly labelled form: a quasi-3D fibronectin sheath (`compute_sheath_channeling`, L452-499) couples Dray et al. (2013) cell-fibronectin loss to tailbud widening and slower elongation through a hoop-equilibrium channeling factor (fn1a;fn1b MO v/v_WT = 0.79 vs ~0.70, width 1.24 vs ~1.20), and a Her1/Her7 clock advection solver (`solve_segmentation_clock_arrest`, L546-640) arrests the clock at a jamming-ratio gate strictly anterior to the fluid-to-solid crossover (161 vs 81 um) and yields Cooke-Zeeman somites of 46.2 um (ref ~47 um; frozen stripe wavelength 46.2 um), with somite fibronectin feeding back into the sheath. Six Lean 4 theorems (2 new), 14 measurements (6 new), 13 self-test checks and 9 DOIs back the script. The score drops slightly because the new modules multiply a still-phenomenological elongation law (prefactor, tanh fluidity, hard-coded asymmetry overrides at L716-728), the new reference values are approximate digitisations, the arrest gate and several clock constants are uncited modelling choices, and row C carries no specimen.

Remediation:
- 2022-tailbud-neuromesodermal-jamming.py:452-499 and 485-486 — `compute_sheath_channeling` converts influx stored in radial dilation into a channeling factor (a transient-window argument) and the dynamic cell-ECM wall friction F_drag is computed but never fed back on v_elong, so the v2 request for ECM boundary FRICTION is only half met. Replace with an axisymmetric thin-film Stokes/Bingham solve with a fibronectin traction boundary condition (zeta_FN f_FN v_rel) so friction and confinement both enter the axial force balance.
- 2022-tailbud-neuromesodermal-jamming.py:146-147 with datasets/neuromesodermal_jamming/ neuromesodermal_jamming_reference.json:269-315 — K_FN = 2800 Pa and K_t = 40 Pa are calibrated to approximate Dray et al. (2013) ratios (fn1a;fn1b 0.70, itga5 0.82, widths 1.20/1.08) and the itga5 regime misses by 16% (0.95 vs 0.82). Digitise the actual Dray 2013 Fig. 2/4 elongation-rate and tissue-width values with panel pins and uncertainties, and anchor the sheath modulus to a measured ECM/tissue stiffness.
- 2022-tailbud-neuromesodermal-jamming.py:521-543 and 156 — the clock arrest gate Lambda_arrest = 4 is an uncited modelling choice; the biological determination front is set by FGF8/Wnt (Dubrulle et al. 2001; cf. 2003-dubrulle-fgf8-wavefront-somitogenesis.py in this repository). Couple x_arrest to an FGF8 gradient threshold and compare the resulting position with her1 in situ / Soroldoni (2014) wave data instead of the P-PSM/A-PSM boundary proxy (ref 180 um).
- 2022-tailbud-neuromesodermal-jamming.py:546-640 — the clock is a phase oscillator with a prescribed omega(x) (floor 0.15/0.85 at L576, c_slow = 0.6 at L159), and the frozen-stripe wavelength check (L615-623, measurement `wt_frozen_her1_stripe_wavelength_um`) is numerically circular with S. Replace the phase model with the Lewis (2003) her1/her7 DDE (or the repository's 2003-lewis script) coupled through the same advection, and validate stripe spacing against measured somite lengths per stage.
- 2022-tailbud-neuromesodermal-jamming.py:716-728 — the elongation law remains a calibrated product (J prefactor 2.034 x backstop x tanh(sigma_A/29) fluidity x chi, ablation special-case 0.41) and the blebbistatin (2.25%) and SU5402 (2.65%) L-R asymmetries are hard-coded overrides; the 5-regime r = 0.9748 therefore partly scores fitted constants. Derive v_elong and the asymmetry from the 2D simulator's own kinematics (tip displacement, left/right length difference) and remove the overrides.
- 2022-tailbud-neuromesodermal-jamming.py:1478-1601 — row C has no vendored specimen: vendor a CC-licensed fibronectin immunostain of the zebrafish tailbud (Dray/Lawton or IDR) and a her1 in situ or Soroldoni (2014) wave image, segment the FN boundary / stripe spacing, and add them as specimen comparisons and measurements (also render the fn1a;fn1b 2D regime, simulated at L1142-1153, as a spatial panel).
