## Findings and remediation

### Bressloff et al. (2001/2002) V1 Euclidean Symmetry-Breaking & Klüver Form Constants  —  overall 79.2 (mostly faithful)

Blinded second-assessor pass. 2001-bressloff-v1-form-constants.py faithfully ports the analytical perturbative dispersion relation, Fourier-Bessel lateral coupling, cubic normal-form overlap integrals, and two-regime log-polar retino-cortical map from MesmerPrism/bressloff-v1-form-constants, reproducing its five stability reports to 2.22e-16 and anchoring linear cortical magnification against Horton and Hoyt (1991) with 6.8% RMS relative error. However, it does not dynamically integrate the Bressloff (2001) V1 integro-differential neural field or compute the Rule et al. (2011) Floquet multipliers in Python, instead rendering static trigonometric superpositions and plotting pre-computed boundary curves from a static JSON fixture. Critically, the retinal pullback folds the horizontal coordinate via abs(ug) inside arctan2(vg, abs(ug)), forcing mirror symmetry across the vertical meridian and turning the oblique spiral roll into achiral heart/chevron cusps. Moreover, all four rendered gallery panels share identical biophysical parameters (q* = 1.4475, k* = 11 cycles) and differ only because the categorical pattern name and angle are manually switched, with three of the four rendered patterns differing from the cubic amplitude-equation selection.
