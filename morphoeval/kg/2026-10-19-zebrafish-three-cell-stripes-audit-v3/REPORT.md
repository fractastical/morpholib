### Nakamasu et al. (2009) 3-variable zebrafish pigment-cell reaction-diffusion network + Volkening-Sandstede iridophore S/L agents on a growing flank, laser-ablation polarity & leopard (cx41.8) spot-to-stripe morphospace  —  overall 90.6 (faithful)

Pass 3 (v3) post-remediation evaluation of `2009-nakamasu-zebrafish-three-cell-stripes.py` (overall weighted score: 90.6 / 100, down 1.5 points from 92.1 in Pass 2 because the round exposed two defects that the Pass 2 scores had credited). The single Pass 2 remediation item is closed: Section 5b couples the Nakamasu (u, v, w) continuum PDE to discrete dense/loose iridophore agents (reduced Volkening & Sandstede 2018 rules) on an isometrically growing flank (stage PB, SL 7.2 mm -> SL 18 mm) with the Crampin-Gaffney-Maini dilution term; the X0 cue now emerges from agents seeded on the myoseptum instead of a whole-flank cos(k_c y) template, stripes are inserted sequentially 2 -> 4 (parallel, anisotropy 30.2; 3/3 seeds aligned) with dense iridophores colonizing the new X1D/X1V interstripes (100% of S agents in interstripes, 68% of L agents in stripes), the iridophore-less control is isotropic (anisotropy 1.01, shady-like) and the static adult-size control is less aligned (8.4) than the growing flank; six new reference-backed measurements, five new self-test checks and Lean Theorems 4-6 (+2 lemmas, 0 sorrys) back the new mathematics. Exposed defects: the v2 wild-type panel and the Fig. 3 regeneration experiment are template-cued over the whole flank (their stripe count/orientation is imposed), the v2 CLI flags were never wired to the computation, the hybrid insertion is symmetric and late (SL 13.5 vs ~10.4-11.0 mm) and depends on a calibrated x-coherent iridophore kernel.
