Remediation:
- 2016-onuff-joint-interzone-clefting.py:334-349 and 364-373 — the 1D RD PDE places its interzones at pre-computed `joint_targets` injected as Gaussian `wave_drive` sources and hard-codes the Gdf5 bp/bp PIP fusion with `if cleft_amp < 0.35 and idx_j == 1: continue`. Replace the template with genuine wavelength selection (PFR-modulated D_G, D_N or kinetic parameters so the Turing wavelength of Law 2 sets the phalanx lengths) and let the bp/bp fusion follow from a Gdf5-dose-dependent cohesion/threshold.
- 2016-onuff-joint-interzone-clefting.py:566-663 — the poroelastic solver is a storage equation with a rigid skeleton; add the solid displacement u and effective stress (Biot u-p, or at least a 1D consolidation column across the interzone) so that the tissue separation and the articular arch arise from strain, not from the parametric rule at L631-633.
- 2016-onuff-joint-interzone-clefting.py:631-640 and 691-751 — the modelled proximal head contour is flat (head sagitta/half-span -0.036) because the Darcy lens thickens at the midline and cancels the imposed arch; the radiograph heads are convex. Either let the cavity reach the capsule periphery (Robin instead of Dirichlet drainage at the perichondrium) or compute the arch from a Carter-Wong growth law so a convex head / concave base pair emerges, and assert head_sagitta_norm > 0 in self-test check 12.
- 2016-onuff-joint-interzone-clefting.py:1019-1023 and 1053 — Digit II's stored centerline has no bone contrast over its distal half (profile ~67 flat), so its DIP minimum is set by the window fraction, and `n_synovial_clefts: 3` is a constant. Re-trace the Digit II centerline through bone, detect the cleft count from the number of radiolucent minima with a contrast gate, and separate the joint space from the physis minimum 10-12 px distal (the specimen is juvenile).
- 2016-onuff-joint-interzone-clefting.py:566-591 and 666 — A_arc_over_W = 0.22, K_identity = 0.30, kappa_G = 0.5, r_cav = 3.0 and the Kahn reversion factor 0.85 are uncited calibrations. Sweep A_arc and kappa_G (parameter-expressivity), and anchor at least kappa_G / sigma_0 to the Edwards 1994 / Maroudas 1976 ratio of interzone cohesion to Donnan pressure, or state them as free in the docstring and reference JSON.
- 2016-onuff-joint-interzone-clefting.py:1598-1619 — the Sox9 reversion ratio and the loading threshold m* carry `reference_value: null`. Replace with a quantitative anchor (e.g. Kahn 2009 Fig. 4 Col2a1 signal in the muscle-less interzone, or Roddy 2011's graded immobilisation series) or move them out of the validation set into a 'predictions' list.
- 2016-onuff-joint-interzone-clefting.py:869-948 — the joint-line arch statistic rests on 4 valid joints with sd 0.078 > mean 0.056. Add a second radiograph (adult, closed physes) or trace the MCP joints as well, and report a bootstrap CI so the model-specimen comparison in check 12 is not a 0.05 tolerance.
- 2016-onuff-joint-interzone-clefting.py:1872-1879 vs 1882 — `--m-load` / `--permeability` only reach `render_cavitation_figure`; Panel C of the comparison figure always uses m_load = 1 and 0. Thread the CLI values through `render_comparison_figure` and add a requirements lock file for the slot.
