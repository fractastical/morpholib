## Findings and remediation

### Bilsborough et al. (2011), Barkoulas et al. (2008), Vlad et al. (2014) & Kierzkowski et al. (2019) growing leaf margin PIN1-Auxin-CUC2-RCO feedback loop: emergent integrated-contour dissection, growing cellular spring-lattice lamina with Mitchison-canalized veins and microtubule-anisotropic growth, and Cardamine hirsuta specimen segmentation  —  overall 68.2 (mostly faithful)

Pass 3 (v3) evaluation of `2011-przemyslaw-allometric-compound-leaf.py` (overall 68.2/100, down from 93.1 in v2). The remediation replaced the Pass 2 closed-form dissection targets with an emergent radial-growth margin integrator and added a growing 2D cellular spring-lattice lamina with Mitchison-canalized lateral veins (every convergence point drains to the midvein in WT, none in cuc2-1) and Hamant-type vein-axis microtubule anisotropy, backed by 2 new machine-checked Lean 4 theorems (5 total, 0 sorrys), 20 reference- anchored measurements and a 12-check self-test. Doing so exposed that v2's 6-genotype r = 0.998 was circular: with one gain calibrated on Arabidopsis WT (0.230 vs 0.228) the mutants emerge smooth (I_D = 0) but Cardamine WT reaches only 0.254 (ref 0.785; sweep ceiling 0.427), mir164a-4 and rco are mis-ordered, and the emergent r is 0.807 (p = 0.052). The margin auxin peaks remain a prescribed template, the benchmark I_D references carry no figure provenance, the specimen "references" are the script's own outputs, the mesh teeth sit at the cell-scale resolution limit and the lamina stress-feedback anisotropy is disabled. The specimen segmentation (32 lobes, I_D 0.5202) is unchanged.
