### 1.1 The 17-Landmark "Stick-Polygon" vs. 600-Point Continuous Silhouette Paradox

For over a century, D'Arcy Wentworth Thompson's coordinate transformation grids (*On Growth and Form*, 1917, Ch. XVII, pp. 748–751, Figs. 373–382) have been the foundational visual icon of mathematical biology. Yet when modern geometric morphometricians tested low-dimensional parametric coordinate grids on standard ichthyological landmark datasets (Claverie & Wainwright 2014; Friedman et al. 2019), global coordinate transformations appeared to fail across families, prompting widespread reliance on exact 34-parameter Thin-Plate Splines (TPS; Bookstein 1989) that interpolate any 17 landmarks with zero residual—a practice Fred Bookstein himself recently critiqued (Bookstein 2023, 2024) as non-falsifiable overfitting.

We formalized all four of Thompson's 1917 fish coordinate families as explicit 5- to 8-parameter differentiable maps composed with an initial affine transformation $(u, v) = (x, y, 1)M$:
1. **Family I — Oblique Shear (5p, Figs. 373–374, *Argyropelecus* $\to$ *Sternoptyx*):** $x' = ax + gy + t_x,\; y' = dy + t_y$.
2. **Family II — Complex Möbius Coaxial Circles (8p, Figs. 375–376, *Scarus* $\to$ *Pomacanthus*):** $x' + iy' = \frac{u + iv}{(c_1 + ic_2)(u + iv) + 1}$.
3. **Family III — Bilinear Fan of Coordinates (8p, Figs. 377–380, *Polyprion* $\to$ *Pristigenys* $\to$ *Scorpaena* $\to$ *Antigonia*):** $x' = u(1 + f_1 v),\; y' = v(1 + f_2 u)$.
4. **Family IV — Hyperbolic / Radial Expansion (8p, Figs. 381–382, *Diodon* $\to$ *Mola mola*):** $x' = u + r_1 v^2,\; y' = v \exp(r_2 u)$.

When we evaluated these four coordinate laws on both the **standard 17 skeletal landmarks** and the **continuous 600-point whole-fish silhouettes**, we uncovered a fundamental methodological paradox:

- In the standard 17-landmark reef-fish protocol, **9 of the 17 landmarks ($53\%$ of the ordinary least-squares weight) lie inside the anterior skull and opercle** ($\mathrm{LM}\,1\text{–}2, 11\text{–}17$), while the remaining **8 landmarks mark only the basal insertion points where the fins attach to the body wall** ($\mathrm{LM}\,3\text{–}10$). **Zero landmarks ($0\%$) lie on the external margins of the dorsal fin, anal fin, caudal tail, or ventral keel.**
- Consequently, when a 5- to 8-parameter coordinate grid (or even an exact 34-parameter Thin-Plate Spline!) is fitted to the 17 landmarks of an interfamily pair such as *Scarus ghobban* $\to$ *Pomacanthus semicirculatus* and the real 600-point fish silhouette is carried along with the grid, **the 17-landmark fit actively degrades the whole-fish silhouette compared to no deformation at all**:
  - On *Scarus* $\to$ *Pomacanthus* (Figs. 375–376), the symmetric Area of Difference $|\Omega_A \Delta \Omega_B| = |\Omega_B \setminus \Omega_A| + |\Omega_A \setminus \Omega_B|$ **worsens from $40.6\%$ (unwarped 4p similarity) to $56.2\%$ of target fish area** (creating a $47.2\%$ blue posterior overhang; $\mathrm{IoU}$ drops from $0.662 \to 0.618$, and drops to $0.629$ even under an exact 34p TPS).
  - On *Scorpaena* $\to$ *Antigonia* (Figs. 379–380), 17-landmark fitting worsens $|\Omega_A \Delta \Omega_B|$ from **$102.2\%$ (unwarped) to $143.7\%$** ($\mathrm{IoU}: 0.417 \to 0.389$).
  - On *Argyropelecus* $\to$ *Sternoptyx* (Figs. 373–374), 17-landmark fitting worsens $|\Omega_A \Delta \Omega_B|$ from **$58.4\%$ (unwarped) to $63.0\%$**.

### 1.2 Direct 600-Point Silhouette Fitting (Anchored on Bookstein's Baseline) Recovers All Six 1917 Pairs

To prevent non-homologous tangential sliding around the perimeter—Bookstein's classic objection to unanchored outline morphometrics—we formulated a direct 600-point contour estimator that minimizes the symmetric bidirectional Chamfer distance between the warped 600-point source contour $\Phi_\theta(B_S)$ and target contour $B_T$, anchored **solely at Bookstein's two axial baseline endpoints** ($\mathrm{LM}\,2$, anterior snout tip $\mathbf{x}_{\mathrm{snout}}$, and $\mathbf{x}_{\mathrm{ped}} = \tfrac{1}{2}(\mathrm{LM}\,5 + \mathrm{LM}\,6)$, caudal peduncle base midpoint, with weight $\lambda_{\mathrm{ax}} = 0.15$):

$$\mathcal{L}_{\mathrm{sil}}(\theta) = \frac{1}{2|B_S|}\sum_{\mathbf{p} \in \Phi_\theta(B_S)} \min_{\mathbf{q} \in B_T} \|\mathbf{p} - \mathbf{q}\|^2 + \frac{1}{2|B_T|}\sum_{\mathbf{q} \in B_T} \min_{\mathbf{p} \in \Phi_\theta(B_S)} \|\mathbf{q} - \mathbf{p}\|^2 + \lambda_{\mathrm{ax}}\!\left(\|\Phi_\theta(\mathbf{x}_{\mathrm{snout}}^S) - \mathbf{x}_{\mathrm{snout}}^T\|^2 + \|\Phi_\theta(\mathbf{x}_{\mathrm{ped}}^S) - \mathbf{x}_{\mathrm{ped}}^T\|^2\right).$$

Zero internal skull landmarks ($\mathrm{LM}\,11\text{–}17$) and zero dorsal/anal fin-base insertions ($\mathrm{LM}\,3\text{–}4, 7\text{–}8$) enter the contour fit. When the **exact same 5- to 8-parameter Thompson coordinate families** are fitted via $\mathcal{L}_{\mathrm{sil}}(\theta)$ across all six of Thompson's 1917 pairs, Thompson's visual transformations are dramatically recovered, with **100.0% orientation-preserving diffeomorphic bijectivity ($\det D\Phi(x,y) > 0$ everywhere, zero grid folds)** and full **reverse-direction ($T \to S$) invertibility**:

### 1.3 The ~45–50 Myr Phylogenetic Horizon & Modular Decoupling Cliff Across 791 Species (4,334 Dated Pairs)

Why does the 17-landmark skeleton obey Thompson's 8-parameter coordinate grids within genera and young families, yet break down across deep interfamily divergences? By linking all 791 Indo-Pacific reef-fish species (4,505 pairs; Claverie & Wainwright 2014; Friedman et al. 2019) to the Rabosky et al. (2018) time-calibrated megaphylogeny (`4,334` dated pairs), we discovered a sharp **$\sim 45\text{–}50\text{ Myr}$ macroevolutionary horizon**:

- **Below $50\text{ Myr}$:** 8-parameter Thompson coordinate grids reduce the 17-landmark Procrustes residual all the way to the target genus's own interspecific biological noise floor in **$53.0\%$** of pairs diverged $0\text{–}10\text{ Myr}$ ($n=149$), **$61.3\%$** at $10\text{–}25\text{ Myr}$ ($n=359$), and **$49.7\%$** at $25\text{–}50\text{ Myr}$ ($n=346$, nearly double the 6p affine rate of $27.5\%$).
- **Above $50\text{ Myr}$ (The Modular Decoupling Cliff):** Noise-floor attainment collapses across a sharp phylogenetic cliff to **$10.6\%$** at $50\text{–}80\text{ Myr}$ ($n=170$), **$1.0\%$** at $80\text{–}110\text{ Myr}$ ($n=618$), and **$0.0\%$** above $110\text{ Myr}$ ($n=2,692$).
- **Node-Disjoint Independent Pairs Control ($n = 187$):** Restricting to strictly node-disjoint independent pairs (where no species or genus is ever reused across pairs, eliminating $100\%$ of pairwise phylogenetic pseudoreplication) yields a continuous logistic inflection horizon of **$t^* = 45.8\text{ Myr}$** ($54.0\%$ reaching the noise floor at $<50\text{ Myr}$ vs. **$0.0\%$** at $\ge 50\text{ Myr}$).
- **Small-Sample $\text{AIC}_c$ Parsimony Sweet Spot ($n = 34$ scalar landmark coordinates):** Across all 4,505 pairs, 8-parameter Thompson grids beat 6-parameter affine maps by $\text{AIC}_c$ in **$71.8\%$** (intrageneric), **$82.2\%$** (intrafamily), and **$76.0\%$** (interfamily) of pairs, while beating Bookstein's 12-parameter quadratic polynomial trend surface in **$83.0\%$**, **$84.9\%$**, and **$92.1\%$** of pairs.

### 1.4 Three Interlocking Causal Mechanisms: Statistical Lever-Arm, Evo-Devo Modularity, and Hydrodynamic Silhouettes

Our double causal ablation study (`thompson/run_rigor_stress_tests.py`, Figure 4) proves *why* internal 17-landmark skeletons break global grids above $50\text{ Myr}$ while whole-fish silhouettes continue to obey them:
1. **The Statistical Lever-Arm Trap ($\mathrm{LM}\,1\text{–}2, 11\text{–}17$):** Placing $53\%$ of the landmarks in the anterior $\sim 25\%$ of the body (the skull and opercle) creates a long geometric lever arm: minor trophic remodeling of the jaws rotates or curves the fitted coordinate grid across the posterior $75\%$ of the fish, producing $587.1\%$ posterior silhouette error on *Scarus* $\to$ *Pomacanthus* when the 9 head landmarks are fitted alone. Even dropping the 7 internal skull points ($\mathrm{LM}\,11\text{–}17$) and fitting the 10 outer trunk points ($\mathrm{LM}\,1\text{–}10$) still fails ($54.5\%$ error on *Scarus* $\to$ *Pomacanthus*; $153.6\%$ on *Scorpaena* $\to$ *Antigonia*) because $\mathrm{LM}\,3\text{–}10$ sit at the basal fin insertions and amputate $100\%$ of the fin webs and ventral keel.
2. **Developmental Modularity (Cranial Neural Crest vs. Somitic Trunk vs. Dermal Fin Rays):** The teleost skull derives from cranial neural crest cells shaped by feeding mechanics, whereas dorsal and anal fin insertions ($\mathrm{LM}\,3\text{–}8$) migrate along somitic myomeres. Post-warp fin-insertion residuals grow from **$1.16\times$** larger than craniofacial residuals within genera ($0.0112 / 0.0098$) to **$1.28\times$** within families ($0.0132 / 0.0099$) and **$1.48\times$** (up to $2.03\times$) across families ($0.0326 / 0.0225$), directly confirming Satterfield, Claverie & Wainwright (2024).
3. **Hydrodynamic Selection on the Unified Wetted Silhouette:** Water does not flow around an internal 8-vertex skeletal trunk polygon—it flows around the continuous outer silhouette of the body plus its dorsal, anal, and caudal fins acting as a single integrated hydrofoil. Because drag, added mass, and turning torque act on the entire wetted lateral contour, deep-bodied fishes (*Pomacanthus*, *Antigonia*, *Sternoptyx*, *Mola*) co-adapt their median fins and body profile as a unified biomechanical envelope—the exact physical field of force D'Arcy Thompson drew in 1917.

### 1.5 Why Thompson's 8-Parameter Coordinate Law Beats "Any Old 600-Point Outline Method"

**Executable Script & Data:** [`thompson/run_wainwright_600pt_and_convergence_tests.py`](../thompson/run_wainwright_600pt_and_convergence_tests.py) $\cdot$ [`thompson_wainwright_600pt_and_convergence.json`](../thompson_wainwright_600pt_and_convergence.json) $\cdot$ [`docs/outreach_fig4_wainwright_600pt_and_convergence.png`](./outreach_fig4_wainwright_600pt_and_convergence.png)

A natural methodological question (raised by Prof. Peter C. Wainwright) is: *when we move from 17 landmarks to a 600-point silhouette, how do we know D'Arcy Thompson's 8-parameter 2D coordinate laws are doing something special, rather than "any old 600-point outline method"?*

To test this cleanly, we held the **600-point continuous fish silhouette input fixed** across all six of Thompson's 1917 pairs and benchmarked **five competing 600-point methods** head-to-head on four orthogonal criteria: (1) silhouette Area of Difference $|\Omega_A \Delta \Omega_B|$, (2) Chamfer boundary loss, (3) **2D tissue bijectivity ($\det D\Phi(x,y) > 0$ everywhere, no grid folding)**, and (4) **zero-shot prediction of the 14 held-out interior skeletal landmarks** (eye, jaw, opercle, pectoral & median fin insertions) when the coordinate map is fitted *solely* to the 600-point outer silhouette:

1. **vs. 1D Elliptic Fourier Descriptors (EFD, $k = 8$ params / 2 harmonics; Kuhl & Giardina 1982):** 1D outline methods parameterize a perimeter curve $(x(s), y(s))$ and possess **no 2D interior coordinate map $\Phi: \mathbb{R}^2 \to \mathbb{R}^2$ at all** (`0%` ability to predict where the eye, opercle, or spine move inside the body).
2. **vs. Standard 600-Point Arc-Length Semilandmark Thin-Plate Splines (600-pt Boundary TPS):** Because standard 600-point semilandmark methods match boundary points by perimeter arc-length ($s \in [0, 1]$), expansion of the dorsal and anal fin lobes in *Pomacanthus*, *Sternoptyx*, *Scorpaena*, *Antigonia*, and *Mola* slides boundary points non-homologously along the contour, **tearing and folding the 2D interior tissue grid on `6 of 6` pairs (`0%` globally bijective, with $\min \det D\Phi$ plunging to $-6.76$ and up to $25.1\%$ of the body inverted!)**, while degrading **zero-shot interior landmark prediction on `6 of 6` pairs** (up to **$2.4\times$ worse RMS**: `0.072` vs. **`0.032`** on *Polyprion* $\to$ *Pristigenys*; `0.107` vs. **`0.045`** on *Pristigenys* $\to$ *Scorpaena*; `0.134` vs. **`0.091`** on *Scorpaena* $\to$ *Antigonia*).
3. **vs. Generic 12-Parameter Quadratic Polynomials Fitted to the 600 Points:** Even a smooth 12-parameter polynomial fitted to the exact same 600-point Chamfer loss **folds the 2D tissue grid ($\det D\Phi \le 0$) on `4 of 6` pairs** (`63.4%–95.4%` bijective, $\min \det D\Phi = -1.35$) and degrades held-out interior landmark prediction by up to **$2.1\times$** (`0.092` vs. **`0.045`** on *Pristigenys* $\to$ *Scorpaena*; `0.148` vs. **`0.091`** on *Scorpaena* $\to$ *Antigonia*; `0.176` vs. **`0.113`** on *Argyropelecus* $\to$ *Sternoptyx*).
4. **vs. 6-Parameter Linear Affine Warps Fitted to the 600 Points:** Adding Thompson's 2 non-linear bending parameters to a 6p affine map cuts Chamfer contour loss by **$16.2\%\text{–}32.2\%$** on the exact same 600 points (reducing $|\Omega_A \Delta \Omega_B|$ from $27.4\% \to \mathbf{22.6\%}$, $\mathrm{IoU} = 0.794$ on *Scarus* $\to$ *Pomacanthus* under multi-start optimization) while maintaining **$100.0\%$ diffeomorphic bijectivity ($\det D\Phi > 0$ on `6/6` pairs)**.

### 1.6 Data-Driven Morphospace Discovery & Convergent vs. Divergent Evolution Across 791 Species

To move beyond hand-picked 1917 equations and connect Thompson's transformations to **empirical morphospace and convergent evolution** across all 791 Indo-Pacific reef-fish species (`48` families, `62` multi-species genera, `4,334` dated pairs), we performed three data-driven analyses:

1. **Unsupervised Data-Driven 8p SVD-Warps vs. Thompson's 1917 Laws (`92.0%` of Theoretical Ceiling):**
   Instead of assuming Thompson's formulas *a priori*, we used **Leave-One-Family-Out Cross-Validation (LOFO-CV)** across all 48 families to learn the optimal data-driven 8-parameter coordinate basis directly from the 791 species (6p linear affine + the top 2 Empirical Non-Affine Singular Vectors of $Y_i - \text{Affine}(M_0 \to Y_i)$). Across all 791 held-out species:
   - **6p Linear Affine** explains **`25.1%` median (`29.3%` mean)** of shape divergence from the consensus ancestor $M_0$.
   - The optimal **8p Data-Driven SVD-Warp Ceiling** explains **`55.3%` median (`53.1%` mean)**.
   - **D'Arcy Thompson's 1917 analytical 8-parameter laws** explain **`50.9%` median (`50.8%` mean)** — capturing **`92.0%` (median) / `95.6%` (mean) of the theoretical data-driven 8-parameter SVD ceiling** without a single data-trained basis vector!
2. **Capturing the Primary Empirical Morphospace Plane (`PC1 + PC2`):**
   Across the 62 reef-fish genera, a 6-parameter linear affine map captures only **`33.1%` of Empirical Morphospace `PC1`** ($34.6\%$ of clade variance) and **`48.9%` of the `(PC1, PC2)` plane**, because `PC1` involves non-linear anterior-to-posterior coordinate fanning of the dorsal and anal fin bases (`LM 3–4, 7–8`). By contrast, **8-parameter Thompson coordinate grids capture `83.4%` of `PC1`, `95.8%` of `PC2`, and `88.6%` of the entire primary `(PC1, PC2)` empirical morphospace plane**.
3. **Separating Convergent Evolution from Divergent Evolution & "Many-to-One" Modularity:**
