## 1. What is predicted, and how

Each species' whole-body planform is encoded by **7 linearised Thompson transformation modes** evaluated at the Procrustes consensus of 743 Indo-Pacific reef-fish species (Claverie & Wainwright 2014) that sit on the Rabosky et al. (2018) time tree: aspect (u,-v); shear (v,u); fan (uv,0); fan (0,uv); radial bow (v^2,0); coaxial Re (u^2-v^2, 2uv); coaxial Im (2uv, -(u^2-v^2)). These 7 modes capture 57.9% of all 17-landmark shape variance (an equal-dimension PCA captures 86.2%). A multivariate evolutionary process is fitted to the 7 mode coefficients on the tree; the state of any lineage at any age is the Gaussian conditional expectation given the sampled living species, with a 7-D predictive ellipsoid. Reference scales: intrageneric disparity sigma_genus = 0.0113 Procrustes RMS; Thompson-7 representation floor = 0.0233; PCA-7 floor = 0.0130.

Overall: 95% ellipsoids contain 87.7% of held-out species; 50% ellipsoids contain 48.8%. The 7 Thompson modes capture 55.5% of the squared-error reduction (relative to the consensus) that full 30-D shape-space phylogenetic prediction achieves.

Reading the table honestly: within 50 Myr the full 30-D model is the most accurate predictor at every horizon (it beats copying the nearest relative, 0.0108 vs 0.0128 at tau<5 Myr and 0.0165 vs 0.0210 at 25–50 Myr), the data-driven PCA-7 basis beats the a-priori Thompson-7 basis, and the Thompson-7 error is bounded below by its representation floor (~0.017) rather than by the phylogenetic signal. Beyond ~50 Myr every phylogenetic predictor collapses to the consensus level: this is the same ~50 Myr horizon found independently in the Thompson-pair work, recovered here from the held-out error curve alone. The Thompson modes' value is interpretability (7 named deformations, condition number 20 vs 201), not accuracy.

How to read this. The competing null is phylogenetic stasis (fossil = living family mean). Under that null 'closer' and 'direction' should each succeed in about half the measurements, so the tallies above are the test statistic; with this many measurements neither outcome is far from the null, and the test is reported as such. Three limits are structural, not tunable: (i) measurement convention — the landmark head length ends at the opercular landmark rather than the opercular-membrane margin and the landmark body depth is the vertical extent of the 17 points, so landmark-based HL/SL runs a few percent lower than a calliper HL/SL and fossil HL values above every living mean partly reflect that; (ii) placement — several Bolca taxa (*Bellwoodilabrus*, *Ruffoichthys*, *Eoleiognathus*) are stem or incertae-sedis forms that need not sit on the lineage to the sampled living species; (iii) the sampled crown of most of these families is younger than 50 Ma, so the 50 Ma prediction is a stem-lineage state that regresses toward the clade ancestor rather than a within-family interpolation. The sharpest single outcome is *Eoplatax papilio*: the engine predicts the ephippid lineage at 50 Ma to have BDmax/SL 0.67 [0.57–0.77], far shallower than living *Platax* (1.02); the fossil measures about 0.80–1.05, outside the stem-state prediction's 95% interval: the stem-state prediction is falsified as stated. Either the extreme disc body of *Platax* was already in place 50 Myr ago (deep-time stasis that the fitted Pagel-lambda process, which regresses stem states toward the clade ancestor, does not anticipate), or *Eoplatax* sits on a side branch closer to crown *Platax* than a stem placement assumes. Both readings are informative.


## 7. Scope and caveats

- Predictions concern the global body planform (7 Thompson modes). The craniofacial/jaw residual is modelled separately and is not part of these predictions.
- A lineage's state at an age is the conditional expectation under the fitted process given the sampled living species: a best estimate with a stated ellipsoid, not a claim about any particular fossil's phylogenetic position.
- Fossil comparisons assume the fossil lies on or near the stem/crown lineage of the named family; fossil landmark digitisation error adds to the reported RMS.
- Section 3 is the error model. Read any prediction's expected landmark error from its horizon there.
