# Dataset: `bos_taurus_turing_wavelength` — *Bos taurus* (Holstein-Friesian cow)

- **Organism (*Ousia*)**: *Bos taurus* (Domestic cow, Holstein-Friesian)
- **Aristotelian Division**: I. Enaima (Vertebrata) → Mammalia (Zootoka)
- **Biophysical Cause (*Poiein*)**: 1. Chemical Turing / RD (I. Reaction-Diffusion & Stripe Splitting)
- **Question**: can a single-wavelength Turing pre-pattern (Murray 1981) account for the black patches on a cow's flank?
- **Specimen**: `bos_taurus_specimen.jpg` — public-domain Holstein cow photograph (Province of Ontario Picture Bureau slide, c.1920; Community Archives of Belleville and Hastings County via [Wikimedia Commons](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Holstein_Cow_(22815045791).jpg)), SHA-256 pinned in `bos_taurus_turing_wavelength_reference.json`.
- **Note on the gallery copy**: `datasets/species_gallery/I_Enaima_Vertebrata/Bos_taurus.jpg` is airway histology, not coat, and is *not* used here.
- **Script**: `models/1981-murray-bos-taurus-turing-wavelength.py` (`--self-test` / `--no-show`)
- **Outputs**: `bos_taurus_turing_wavelength_metrics.json`, `bos_taurus_turing_wavelength_comparison.png` (generated by the script)
- **Loop record**: `morphoeval/runs/2026-10-26-bos-taurus-turing-wavelength-loop.md`
- **Specimens (Pass 6)**: 10 Holsteins total — the c.1920 specimen above plus 9 held-out Commons photographs (`bos_taurus_specimen_02…10.jpg`; titles, creators, licences and SHA-256 in the reference JSON).
- **Headline result**: on the first flank, a static single-wavelength Turing pattern makes many uniform spots where the cow carries one body-scale patch. After remediation (growing-domain RD fit; melanoblast/Eden positive control; Fable-corrected scoring on unfitted statistics only), the held-out verdict across 10 cows is: RD can fit patch *areas*, but patch *boundaries* (roughness, size diversity) are consistently melanoblast-like (Eden wins 7/9 held-out cows; the ranking is not yet statistically significant, p≈0.2 — see Pass 7). Consistent with the melanoblast-migration (MITF/KIT) account of cattle white spotting.
