# Dataset: `bos_taurus_turing_wavelength` — *Bos taurus* (Holstein-Friesian cow)

- **Organism (*Ousia*)**: *Bos taurus* (Domestic cow, Holstein-Friesian)
- **Aristotelian Division**: I. Enaima (Vertebrata) → Mammalia (Zootoka)
- **Biophysical Cause (*Poiein*)**: 1. Chemical Turing / RD (I. Reaction-Diffusion & Stripe Splitting) — **tested, not supported (see claim below)**

## Claim under test

> **"The black-and-white spotting of Holstein-Friesian cattle is laid down by a Turing reaction–diffusion pre-pattern (Murray 1981) with a characteristic wavelength."**

**Verdict: negative.** This dataset exists to publish that tested negative hypothesis and its assessment — a negative result, cleanly measured, is good science.

- A static single-wavelength Turing pattern is **rejected**: it produces ~10² quasi-uniform spots at \(\lambda_c \approx 10\) px, while the cows carry a few body-scale patches (specimen 01: 3 patches, 99.2% of pigment in one). Held-out morphometric distance 6.46 vs ~3.3 for body-scale alternatives.
- A growing-domain RD variant **cannot be distinguished** from stochastic melanoblast colonisation (Eden-process control) on adult patch statistics once measurement scale is matched (Pass 8: RD 3.29 vs Eden 3.43 across 9 held-out cows; paired Wilcoxon p = 0.91). Adult coat morphology alone is therefore **not evidence for** a Turing mechanism.
- This agrees with established developmental genetics: white spotting is melanoblast colonisation failure. Causal KIT/MITF regulatory variants are confirmed for Holstein spotting ([Jivanji et al. 2025](https://doi.org/10.1126/sciadv.adt5913); [GWAS, Jivanji et al. 2019](https://doi.org/10.1186/s12711-019-0506-2)), and mammalian spotting is modelled quantitatively as stochastic migration + proliferation, not a Turing instability ([Mort et al. 2016](https://doi.org/10.1038/ncomms10288)). No activator–inhibitor morphogen pair has been identified in cattle skin. Murray's 1981 RD pre-pattern proposal was a mathematical hypothesis; it is not established for cow coat.

**Claim boundaries.** This loop does *not* claim: that all RD-family models are excluded (a growing-domain RD is statistically indistinguishable here); that the Eden control is the true mechanism (it is a minimal colonisation proxy); or that patch statistics could ever identify a mechanism alone. The independent (Fable) assessors scored the *package* twice (54.4, 57.7) and their criticisms overturned two builder conclusions (Passes 4, 7–8); no independent re-score of the Pass 8+ tree exists yet (audit remediation R11).
