# Causal Emergence Corpus (`datasets/causal_emergence/`)

Formal empirical datasets, transition probability matrices (TPMs), network benchmarks, Boolean gene-regulatory tables, and biological specimen photographs for replicating Erik P. Hoel's Causal Emergence literature (`2013–2026`).

## Primary Literature Covered

1. **Hoel, Albantakis & Tononi (2013)** — *Quantifying causal emergence shows that macro can beat micro.* Proc. Natl. Acad. Sci. USA 110(49):19790–19795. [doi:10.1073/pnas.1314922110](https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.1314922110)
2. **Hoel, Albantakis, Marshall & Tononi (2016)** — *Can the macro beat the micro? Integrated information across spatiotemporal scales.* Neuroscience of Consciousness 2016(1):niw012. [doi:10.1093/nc/niw012](https://doi.org/10.1093/nc/niw012)
3. **Hoel (2017)** — *When the Map Is Better Than the Territory.* Entropy 19(5):188. [doi:10.3390/e19050188](https://doi.org/10.3390/e19050188)
4. **Griebenow, Klein & Hoel (2019)** — *Finding the right scale of a network: efficient identification of causal emergence through spectral clustering.* arXiv:1908.07565. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1908.07565](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1908.07565)
5. **Klein & Hoel (2020)** — *The Emergence of Informative Higher Scales in Complex Networks.* Complexity 2020:8932526. [doi:10.1155/2020/8932526](https://doi.org/10.1155/2020/8932526)
6. **Hoel & Levin (2020)** — *Emergence of informative higher scales in biological systems: a computational toolkit for optimal prediction and control.* Communicative & Integrative Biology 13(1):108–118. [doi:10.1080/19420889.2020.1802914](https://doi.org/10.1080/19420889.2020.1802914)
7. **Klein, Hoel, Swain, Griebenow & Levin (2021)** — *Evolution and emergence: higher order information structure in protein interactomes across the tree of life.* Integrative Biology 13(12):283–294. [doi:10.1093/intbio/zyab020](https://doi.org/10.1093/intbio/zyab020)
8. **Varley & Hoel (2022)** — *Emergence as the conversion of information: a unifying theory.* Phil. Trans. R. Soc. A 380(2227):20210150. [doi:10.1098/rsta.2021.0150](https://doi.org/10.1098/rsta.2021.0150)
9. **Comolatti & Hoel (2025)** — *Consilience in Causation: Causal Emergence Is Found Across Measures of Causation.* Entropy 27(8):825. [doi:10.3390/e27080825](https://doi.org/10.3390/e27080825)
10. **Hoel (2026)** — *Quantifying emergent complexity (Causal Emergence 2.0).* Patterns 7(1):101472 (`arXiv:2503.13395`, `PyMergence` Zenodo [doi:10.5281/zenodo.17210078](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.17210078)). [doi:10.1016/j.patter.2025.101472](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.patter.2025.101472)
