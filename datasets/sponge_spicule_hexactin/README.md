# Sponge Spicule Silicatein Axial Symmetry & Concentric Lamellar Lattice Reference Dataset (`slot-076`)

Reference dataset and empirical specimen assets for **Uriz, Turon, Becerro & Agell (2003)** *Microscopy Research and Technique* 62(4):279-299 (`10.1002/jemt.10395`), **Aizenberg et al. (2005)** *Science* 309(5732):275-278 (`10.1126/science.1112255`), **Weaver et al. (2007)** *Journal of Structural Biology* 158(1):93-106 (`10.1016/j.jsb.2007.01.006`), and **Shimizu et al. (1998)** *PNAS* 95(11):6234-6238 (`10.1073/pnas.95.11.6234`).

## Files & Provenance

- `sponge_spicule_hexactin_reference.json` — Governing silicatein axial filament ray symmetry laws ($O_h$ 6-ray hexactins at $\theta = 90^\circ$, $T_d$ 4-ray tetractins/calthrops at $\theta = \arccos(-1/3) = 109.4712^\circ$, $D_{3h}$ 3-ray triactines at $\theta = 120^\circ$, and $D_{\infty h}$ 2-ray monaxons at $\theta = 180^\circ$), concentric lamellar silica thickness scaling $h_n = h_1 (r_1 / r_n) \propto r_n^{-1}$, checkerboard diagonal lattice shear stiffness $K_{\text{shear}}(f_{\text{diag}})$, and a 12-species cross-taxa Porifera dataset.
