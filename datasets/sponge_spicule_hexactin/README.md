# Sponge Spicule Silicatein Axial Symmetry & Concentric Lamellar Lattice Reference Dataset (`slot-076`)

Reference dataset and empirical specimen assets for **Uriz, Turon, Becerro & Agell (2003)** *Microscopy Research and Technique* 62(4):279-299 (`10.1002/jemt.10395`), **Aizenberg et al. (2005)** *Science* 309(5732):275-278 (`10.1126/science.1112255`), **Weaver et al. (2007)** *Journal of Structural Biology* 158(1):93-106 (`10.1016/j.jsb.2007.01.006`), and **Shimizu et al. (1998)** *PNAS* 95(11):6234-6238 (`10.1073/pnas.95.11.6234`).

## Files & Provenance

- `sponge_spicule_hexactin_reference.json` — Governing silicatein axial filament ray symmetry laws ($O_h$ 6-ray hexactins at $\theta = 90^\circ$, $T_d$ 4-ray tetractins/calthrops at $\theta = \arccos(-1/3) = 109.4712^\circ$, $D_{3h}$ 3-ray triactines at $\theta = 120^\circ$, and $D_{\infty h}$ 2-ray monaxons at $\theta = 180^\circ$), concentric lamellar silica thickness scaling $h_n = h_1 (r_1 / r_n) \propto r_n^{-1}$, checkerboard diagonal lattice shear stiffness $K_{\text{shear}}(f_{\text{diag}})$, and a 12-species cross-taxa Porifera dataset.
- `euplectella_aspergillum_specimen.jpg` — Public-domain photograph of the Venus' flower basket glass sponge (*Euplectella aspergillum*, Hexactinellida) cylindrical biosilica lattice showing orthogonal hexactin primary struts ($\approx 89.65^\circ$) and alternating diagonal bracing ($\approx 47.68^\circ$, `File:Glass-sponge, Euplectella aspergillum.jpg`, Wikimedia Commons, Public domain, SHA-256: `b56275f43e5ea078d23a79d7f06d01057879d26518730dce7f9dbd7e4f1f9cf3`).
- `scout_manifest.json` — OpenAlex and Wikimedia Commons literature/specimen scout dossier (`fb6e06653d9d14ac34b7910b82899690646b084a4986fee16c41fa0fa5cae39f`).
- `paper_citations.bib` — BibTeX citations for Uriz et al. (2003), Aizenberg et al. (2005), Weaver et al. (2007), and Shimizu et al. (1998) (`81182a1384d11f9388a16c20b909ccd6830ae5225ef29e4c47a76cf025e244cd`).

## Pass 3 additions (2026-10-19, remediation of the v2 audit)

- `cohesive_bending_waveguide_reference.json` — constants and provenance tags for the three Pass 3 models: (i) finite-element three-point bending of the concentric laminate with a bilinear cohesive-zone organic interlayer (He & Hutchinson deflection criterion $G_i/G_s < 0.25$, Hillerborg length $l_{cz} = E G_i / T_{max}^2 = 27\,\mu$m, Griffith interface delamination solved on the FE stiffness), coupled to the 3D silicatein solver through the accreted biosilica cross-section; (ii) external helical surface ridges on the cylindrical cage ($K_{tors} \propto \sin^2 2\psi$, parallel-axis gain $1 + N_r A_r/2$); (iii) basalia step-index optical waveguiding ($NA$, $V$-number, LP-mode count from Bessel zeros). Every constant carries a `provenance` tag (`model` / `recalled` / `exact`); the `recalled` values (biosilica modulus 36 GPa, refractive indices 1.46/1.44, Aizenberg 2005 lamella thicknesses 1.5 → 0.2 µm) and the new DOIs were **not** verified against the papers offline and must be treated as unsourced until checked. The `silica_mass_to_layer_calibration` constant is self-referential by construction (wild-type → 25 layers).
- `pass3_citations.bib` — BibTeX for Sundar et al. (2003), Aizenberg et al. (2004), Woesz et al. (2006), He & Hutchinson (1989), Hillerborg et al. (1976), Gloge (1971). The SHA-256-pinned `paper_citations.bib` is untouched.
- Known geometry mismatch carried to Pass 4: the Law-3 lamellar model (r_core = 0.75 µm, h_1 = 0.85 µm, N = 25) has an outer radius of 5.83 µm, whereas the 12-species table lists 22.5 µm for *Euplectella aspergillum*; the bending model inherits the small radius (span = 20 diameters = 233 µm).
