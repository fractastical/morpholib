## Governing Mathematical & Biomechanical Framework

A mammalian bronchial airway (*Bos taurus* / *Homo sapiens*) is modeled as a concentric cylindrical bilayer enclosed by contractile Airway Smooth Muscle (ASM):
- Inner stiff epithelial/reticular-basement-membrane **mucosa** of thickness $h_m$, shear modulus $\mu_m$, and inner luminal radius $R_0$.
- Outer compliant **submucosal foundation** of thickness $h_s$ and shear modulus $\mu_s \ll \mu_m$.

Under multiplicative morphoelastic decomposition $\mathbf{F} = \mathbf{F}_e \mathbf{F}_g$ driven by ASM circumferential shortening strain $\varepsilon_\theta \in [0, 0.45]$ or mucosal inflammatory swelling $g_m > 1$:

1. **Circumferential Rosette Wavenumber Selection Law:**
   $$\lambda_c = 2\pi h_m \left(\frac{\mu_m}{3\mu_s}\right)^{1/3}, \qquad m^* = \frac{R_0}{h_m}\left(\frac{3\mu_s}{\mu_m}\right)^{1/3}.$$
   - **Healthy thin-walled bronchiole** ($h_m/R_0 \approx 0.018$, $\mu_m/\mu_s \approx 60$): Buckles into numerous shallow folds ($m^* \approx 14$), preserving open luminal area.
   - **Chronic bronchitis** (mucosal edema, $h_m/R_0 \approx 0.032$, $\mu_m/\mu_s \approx 45$): Intermediate fold count ($m^* \approx 9$) with moderate period-doubling.
   - **Asthmatic airway remodeling** (subepithelial fibrosis & reticular basement membrane thickening, $h_m/R_0 \approx 0.052$, $\mu_m/\mu_s \approx 38$): Buckles into few deep inward-protruding folds ($m^* \approx 5\text{–}6$) that undergo secondary period-doubling ($m \to m/2$) and sulcal self-contact creasing.

2. **Inextensible Mucosal Perimeter & Lumen-Area Occlusion Law:**
   For fixed mucosal basement-membrane perimeter $P_0 = 2\pi R_0$ under ASM ring constriction $\varepsilon_\theta$, lower mode number $m$ forces larger radial fold intrusion amplitude $a_m \propto R_0 \sqrt{\varepsilon_\theta} / m$, reducing open luminal area:
   $$A_{\text{lumen}}(\varepsilon_\theta, m) = A_{\text{ASM}}(\varepsilon_\theta) - A_{\text{wall}} - \frac{P_0^2 \Delta_{\text{fold}}(\varepsilon_\theta)}{8\pi m}.$$

3. **Hagen–Poiseuille Quartic Radius / Quadratic Area Airflow Resistance Law:**
   $$\frac{R_{\text{flow}}}{R_{\text{flow},0}} = \left(\frac{A_0}{A_{\text{lumen}}(\varepsilon_\theta, m)}\right)^2,$$
   amplifying airway resistance by $>15\times$ in severe asthmatic remodeling compared to $\sim 2.6\times$ in normal bronchioles at identical ASM shortening.
