# T1917-031 — Thompson (1917) Ch. XIII: horns as logarithmic spirals wound on a cone

Slot 225 of the Thompson-1917 claim-validation campaign (`thompson1917-t1917-031`).

**Claim (dated 1917, pp. 612-634, Figs. 318-324):** horns (ram, goat, *Ovis ammon*) are
logarithmic spirals wound on a cone — a conical helicoid generated by gnomonic growth —
differing between species by the spiral constants (α) and the torsion / cone component (β).

## Dataset verification (Step 1 override)

The registry (`thompson/thompson_1917_dataset_audit.json`) names Dryad `doi:10.5061/dryad.qz612jmb4`
(Tidière et al. 2020). It was fetched from `https://datadryad.org/api/v2/versions/73042/download`
(zip SHA-256 `76d63f67…264c2d`) and inspected: 1887 rows of `species, population, original_study,
sex, body_mass_kg, horn_length_cm, N_BM, N_HL`. **Species match** (*Ovis gmelini*, *Ovis aries*,
*Capra ibex*, …) but **no shape variables** — it is a length-mass allometry table and cannot test a
spiral geometry claim. It is therefore labelled `DATASET_MISMATCH_FOR_SHAPE`, kept only as a pinned
non-shape analogue, and the verdict is driven by the second override option: Thompson's own 1917
figures from the pinned IA scan `ongrowthform1917thom` (printed page = leaf − 20, verified against
`ongrowthform1917thom_page_numbers.json`) plus a CC-licensed photograph of a real *Ovis ammon* horn.

## Model (from the text; see reference.json)

- E1 `ρ(θ) = a e^{kθ}`, `k = cot α`  — plane logarithmic spiral (Ch. XI).
- E2 `ρ = a e^{θ sinβ cotα}`, `z = ρ cotβ`  — spiral wound on a cone of semi-angle β (Ch. XI p. 518, 558).
- E3 `R = e^{2π sinβ cotα}` — whorl ratio.
- E4 `w = c ρ` — gnomonic generating curve (p. 618).
- E5 constant ratio of basal growth velocities ⇒ log spiral; equal ⇒ straight (pp. 616, 619-620).
- Thompson's numbers (p. 617): *Ovis ammon* α ≈ 75°, Highland ram a little more, Scinde wild goat < 70°, Oryx 20-40°.

## Tests performed by the script

`thompson/claims/t1917-031_the-shapes-of-horns-and-of-teeth-or-tusks-with-a.py`

1. Outer horn edge digitised from each image (ink threshold + hole fill for drawings; HSV
   saturation/value mask for the photograph); farthest silhouette point per angular bin inside the
   documented horn sector (annotations in reference.json).
2. E1 fitted with a free pole by nonlinear least squares; α, whorl ratio, Pearson r of ln ρ vs θ, RMSE.
3. Model comparison by AIC against an Archimedean spiral and a circle (the no-gnomon null).
4. Pass 2 adds: the real Argali specimen, *Capra sinaitica*, inner-edge/breadth profiles for the
   gnomonic test (E4, projection-invariant: `k_breadth` must equal `k_edge`), bootstrap CIs, a KS test
   on standardised residuals, synthetic positive/negative controls and an ablation, an α-β regime
   sweep, and the Dryad analogue summary.

- **(a) Log-spiral form:** on both *Ovis ammon* sources the logarithmic spiral beats the circle null
  decisively and is not beaten by the Archimedean spiral → supported.
- **(b) Thompson's α ≈ 75°:** the real specimen reproduces it (74.7°, CI brackets 75°); Thompson's own
  drawing projects at 84°, consistent with 75° only on a cone of semi-angle ≈ 22° (α, β not separable in 2D).
- **(c) Gnomonic self-similarity (E4):** fails on Fig. 320 — breadth decays with |k| = 0.32 while the outer
  edge has |k| = 0.13 (the horn tapers 2.5× faster than its spiral tightens); inconclusive on the photo
  (breadth along rays shows no exponential trend at all, r = 0.04: the trihedral section rotates along the
  whorl — Thompson's torsion — so projected breadth is not a clean proxy for the generating curve).
- **(d) Species contrast:** the ibex half-whorl does not pin α (53–86° depending on sector); Thompson's
  "goat < 70°" cannot be confirmed or refuted from Fig. 319.
- Controls: synthetic α = 75° recovered to 75.0° (pole error 0.1 px); noisy circle classified as circle
  (|k| = 0.0007); shuffling Fig. 320 radii against angles collapses r from 0.97 to 0.01.
- Dryad analogue (non-shape): *Capra ibex* ♂ horn-length ∝ mass^1.23 (r = 0.96), ♀ ∝ mass^1.00 (r = 0.93) — reported only.

**Verdict: `mixed`** — the spiral *form* is supported and the real *Ovis ammon* horn matches Thompson's
spiral constant, but strict gnomonic self-similarity is not, and the cone/torsion component remains
untested without 3D data.

## Known limitations

- A lateral 2D view measures only `k_proj = sinβ cotα`; α and β are not separately identifiable
  (Lean theorem `alpha_beta_not_identifiable_from_projection`). Thompson's α ≈ 75° is consistent
  with the measured projected pitch only for an acute cone (β ≈ 15-35°); 3D centrelines (CT /
  MorphoSource) are needed to close this.
- The Argali photograph is not an orthographic axial view; perspective and the lateral flare of the
  tip are uncorrected.
- Torsion / St Venant ring warping (C5) and inter-species β differences (C3) are not tested.
- n = 1 real specimen; the other two sources are Thompson's own figures (one a halftone of a drawing).
