# Mollusc Shell Marginal Buckling & Rib Morphogenesis Reference Dataset

## Overview

This dataset vendors the mathematical, biomechanical, and empirical morphometric reference parameters for **Slot 072 (`slot-072-mollusc-shell-marginal-buckling`)**, modeling the mechanical morphogenesis and convergent evolution of antimarginal radial ribs, orthomarginal concentric rugae, and hollow marginal spines in mollusc and brachiopod shells:

1. **Chirat, R., Moulton, D. E., & Goriely, A. (2013).** *Mechanical basis of morphogenesis and convergent evolution of spiny seashells.* Proceedings of the National Academy of Sciences, 110(15), 6015–6020. DOI: [`10.1073/pnas.1220443110`](https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.1220443110)
2. **Erlich, A., Moulton, D. E., Goriely, A., & Chirat, R. (2016).** *The physical basis of mollusk shell chiral coiling and antimarginal rib ornamentation.* Journal of the Royal Society Interface, 13(123), 20160592. DOI: [`10.1098/rsif.2016.0592`](https://doi.org/10.1098/rsif.2016.0592)
3. **Moulton, D. E., Goriely, A., & Chirat, R. (2012).** *Mechanical growth and morphogenesis of seashells.* Journal of Theoretical Biology, 311, 69–79. DOI: [`10.1016/j.jtbi.2012.07.009`](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jtbi.2012.07.009)
4. **Rudraraju, S., Moulton, D. E., Chirat, R., & Goriely, A. (2019).** *A computational framework for the morpho-elastic development of molluskan shells by surface and volume growth.* PLoS Computational Biology, 15(7), e1007213. DOI: [`10.1371/journal.pcbi.1007213`](https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pcbi.1007213)

## Governing Equations

Along the generative shell aperture margin $s \in [0, L(R)]$, the mantle edge acts as a growing elastic beam of flexural rigidity $B = E I$ attached to the previously calcified shell margin (elastic foundation stiffness $k$, cubic stiffening $\kappa_3$) under compressive axial force $P$ generated by circumferential growth excess $\gamma = (L_m - L_s)/L_s > 0$:

$$E I \frac{\partial^4 w}{\partial s^4} + P \frac{\partial^2 w}{\partial s^2} + k w + \kappa_3 w^3 = 0$$

- **Critical Buckling Load:** $P_c = 2\sqrt{E I k}$
- **Critical Wavenumber & Antimarginal Rib Wavelength:**
  $$q_c = \left(\frac{k}{E I}\right)^{1/4}, \qquad \lambda_c = \frac{2\pi}{q_c} = 2\pi \left(\frac{E I}{k}\right)^{1/4}$$
- **Supercritical Pitchfork Rib Amplitude ($P > P_c$):**
  $$A_{\mathrm{eq}}(P) = \sqrt{\frac{4 q_c^2 (P - P_c)}{3 \kappa_3}}$$
- **Rib Number Scaling Along Shell Radius $R$ (Fan Angle $\Phi_0$):**
  $$N_{\mathrm{ribs}} = \frac{\Phi_0 R}{\lambda_c} = \frac{\Phi_0 R}{2\pi} \left(\frac{k}{E I}\right)^{1/4}$$
